# CLAUDE.md

This file provides guidance to Claude Code (claude.ai/code) when working with code in this repository.

## What this is

A browser car-maneuvering trainer (parking simulator) — one self-contained file, `index.html` (~5700 lines): CSS, HTML UI, and a single `<script>` with zero dependencies, no build step, no package.json; git-репозиторий на `main` без feature-веток (плюс генерат `codegraph-src/index.js` для индекса — см. Code Search). All UI text and code comments are in Russian — keep new text and comments in Russian. Testing harness: the headless demo regression (`computeIdealPath` runs on every `loadLevel` — intercept `console.warn` over all `DEMOS` keys, zero timeouts = green) plus `learner.js` (см. ниже) for the teaching texts.

### Перед деплоем — коммит и пуш (обязательно)

На сервер уезжает ровно то, что лежит в репозитории. Деплой из грязного дерева кладёт на прод код,
которого нет в git: его не воспроизвести, не отревьюить и не откатить. Все ручные деплой-скрипты
проверяют это сами и падают до первой заливки — грязное дерево или неотправленные коммиты
останавливают деплой. Аварийный обход `SKIP_GIT_GUARD=1` оставляет предупреждение в логе.
Деплой через GitHub Actions это правило соблюдает по построению: CI собирает из запушенного коммита.

## Architecture of index.html

Layout: CSS first (~190 lines), then HTML panels/overlays/touch controls, then one `"use strict"` script to the end. The script is organized in commented sections (`/* ---------- name ---------- */`) in dependency order; new code goes into the matching section.

**Coordinate system** (comment at ~line 264): all physics runs in the 2D ground plane `(u, v)`; the 3D world maps as `x = -u`, `z = v`, `y = up`. `yaw 0` points along `+v` ("north"), yaw grows turning right. Converters: `fuv`/`ruv` (2D orts) and `fwd`/`rgt` (3D).

**Software 3D renderer on Canvas 2D**: `setCam`/`toCam`/`toScreen` + near-plane clipping (`clipNear`, `NEAR` = 0.08 m — the headliner is 0.16 m above the eye, and the old 0.22 m plane cut it away when looking up, showing sky through the roof; a camera must never sit within 0.08 m in front of geometry it should not see, which is why the side-mirror cameras sit *inside* their housings at the rear face), `screenToGround` for the inverse (it reads the `viewCam` snapshot taken in `render`, because by the time a pointer event fires `cam` holds the last mirror's parameters), and a painter's-algorithm face list — geometry is emitted via `pushFace`/`pushBox`/`pushQuad`/`pushPoly` with lambert shading (`shadeCol`), then depth-sorted and drawn in `flushFaces`. The whole scene renders through `drawSceneInto`, which is reused for the main view and for mirrors (`renderMirror`) in cockpit mode. Painter's-sort rules paid for: (1) the sort key is the **distance from the camera to the centroid of the visible, near-clipped polygon**, not the depth along the view axis — depth changes with every degree of head rotation, so from the cockpit the panel order re-shuffled while looking around and the cabin flickered and «lost» panels; distance is rotation-invariant, and a face crossing the near plane is measured by its clipped part (the plain vertex mean of a wall beside the car went to ~0 and the wall was painted over the cabin); (2) inside the cabin the scene is two passes — world, flush, then own body + interior — because nothing outside can be nearer than the interior trim; mirrors and outside views stay single-pass so the own body sorts against neighbours; (3) a small detail sitting on a big face (exterior door handles and seams, door-card trim) carries a `bias` in metres that sorts it nearer — the host face's centroid is often nearer than the detail — and `pushFace/pushQuad/pushPoly/pushBox/pushBar` all take it; (4) interpenetrating boxes cannot be sorted at all (the seat back drew through the cushion, the visor through the roof header) — cabin parts sit on each other, never inside; (5) long world boxes are split into 3.5 m segments in `buildRenderList` (a 7 m kerb had its centroid nearer than the neighbour's wheel in front of it). `tools/sort-audit.js` → `sortAudit({yaws:[0,-90,135]})` in the page console counts wrong-order overlapping face pairs per pass (`[FIX:sort]`); it found all of the above — run it after touching geometry, the target is 0 in the interior pass. Three more per-frame rules in `flushFaces`/`pathCam`: **faces are not stroked** — `pathCam` pushes every screen vertex outward along the bisector of its edge normals by 0.9 **device** px (`EXPAND_DEV/pxScale` — 0.5 left a light seam on GPU Canvas at DPR 2, two half-covered edges; 0.9 is what the old 0.9 px stroke gave; `pxScale` is the DPR of whichever canvas is current, swapped for mirrors), damped for faces thinner than 1.5 px so edge-on loft slivers don't fatten into lines, orientation from the signed screen area — in screen coordinates (y down) a positive shoelace area is clockwise and `(dy, −dx)` points outward; with the sign inverted every face shrank and the world turned into a wireframe of gaps («всё в линиях»). This closes the anti-aliasing hairlines between neighbours (sky showed through them and shimmered) at zero extra raster passes — the old fill+stroke per face was half the frame time on a software Canvas; faces under 1 px² are skipped (far cars had hundreds). `cockpit-shots` guards it with `skyGap` (sky-coloured pixels in the dash band of the forward frame, live `getImageData`, limit 60; stroke gave 10, expansion 12 headless / 19 GPU, the inverted sign 36); and a cabin face may carry a second `fill()` of the same path after the colour fill — `grainFace` (material grain: a semi-transparent noise tile mapped through `ctx.transform` in the face's own metres→pixels frame, no `clip`, no `overlay` — both were dearer than the fill itself) and `imgFace` (a pre-rendered canvas as a `no-repeat` pattern mapped by three corners: speedometer, console screen, window switches, speaker grille), see **Cabin lighting**. What a face gets is decided by the pure `faceMode(f, s0..s3)`: mirrors are always flat; a gradient needs its two endpoints ≥ 1.5 px apart (a **degenerate `createLinearGradient` paints nothing** — edge-on faces vanished for a frame, «текстуры моргают») and a face ≥ `GRAD_MIN_PX` = 40 px, otherwise the mid colour `colMid`; grain fades continuously with screen area and tile minification (`f.gk`, thresholds would flicker); endpoints come from `toScreenClamped` (depth clamped to `NEAR`) so a face crossing the near plane never jumps between modes. `tools/cockpit-shots.mjs --sweep` turns the head −60…+60° by 1° on three pitches and counts mode toggles and degenerate gradients — the target is 0/0. `pushBar` cuts long bars into `seg` pieces with end caps only at the real ends — a cap on an inner joint showed through the neighbouring piece as a grey post.

**Car physics**: `CAR` const holds all vehicle dimensions/limits (kinematic bicycle model, Ackermann steering — `ackermann`, `sweep`). `stepCar` integrates at a fixed 1/120 s substep inside `frame`. Steering rate is speed-dependent (`steerRateNow`). The canonical pose is the rear axle (`car.ru/rv/th`); body center comes from `bodyPos()`/`setBody()` via `C2R`. Gear selector `P/R/N/D` lives in `car.sel`, drive direction in `car.gear`. `shiftSel` enforces a real automatic's rules: brake-shift interlock (leaving `P` requires `input.back`, i.e. S or Space held) and full stop before P/R/D — both refuse with a HUD warning (`selWarn`, `selBlockT`; touch also highlights the brake button via `body.needbrake`). Space is a second brake key, not a handbrake. `selectGear(target)` walks the lever to a position and reverts to the starting one if it can't get there, so a single command never strands the car in `N`. Four ways to shift, all through those same rules: `Enter` (D ⇄ R — the constant move while parking), `P`, `,`/`.` one position at a time, and clicking the P R N D letters in the HUD (`#gearVal span[data-gear]`) or the touch `#tgear` buttons.

**Self-centering** (the wheel returning on its own) uses the real law — self-aligning torque grows with the *square* of speed: `back = min(casterMax, casterGain*(v² − casterV²))`, gain `rad(2.2)` per (m/s)², cap `rad(50)`. Keeping the `casterV` threshold at 2.2 m/s is what makes the feature safe: below ~8 km/h there is no return at all, so parking maneuvers, the level hints that say "hold full lock", and the calibration of all 11 demos are untouched, while at 15–19 km/h the wheel unwinds in 0.7 s. Measured: 9 km/h → 11 s to unwind, 11 km/h → 3.7 s, 14 km/h → 1.4 s, 19 km/h → 0.7 s. **A linear-in-speed law cannot be used** — it already unwinds the wheel at parking pace and breaks parallel parking. `demoStep` holds the wheel only while `|err| > 1.5°` and *brakes in that same condition*, so a caster that drifts the wheel inside the dead band would make demos stutter between "correcting" and "driving": the narrow 0.35° trim band exists for that, and it is gated on `|vel| > casterV` — widening it below the threshold changes the arc radius and rips up the segment calibration (it cost L11 a failed run before being gated).

Full lock is 34.5° at the wheels = **1.49 turns** of the steering wheel (`steerRatio` 15.5); `atLock()` marks the stop, which colours the rim mark red and clicks `tone()` **on the edge** (`lockWas`), never per frame.

**Collision & clearances**: two-phase. The OBB SAT with minimum translation vector (`satMTV`) is only the **broad phase**; the contact itself is decided on the car's real ground footprint — `CAR_HULL`, the convex hull of the loft's stations — against the obstacle's shape (`polyMTV`, generic convex SAT; a neighbouring car is the same hull, everything else a rectangle). The OBB is 4.42 × 1.80 while the body tapers at both ends (the nose at z=2.21 is 1.48 m wide, the tail 1.56 m), so the rectangle's corners hung outside the metal and a touch was counted **2–9 cm before the bodywork met anything** — 1.7 cm at 6° of yaw, 5.5 cm at 20°, 9.2 cm at 35°, and 13.5 cm car-to-car at 45°. That is what «габариты кривые спереди и сзади, по текстурам ещё не задел, а пишет соприкосновение» meant. The narrow phase runs **only** on obstacles the broad phase already flagged, so the per-frame cost is a rounding error (+0.1 ms on the exam scene). `drawRefs` and `emitCornerPosts` draw that same hull — the frame and the posts may never drift from what a hit is measured against — and the posts sit on the hull vertex furthest in each diagonal (front corners at z=2.12, rear at z=−2.06), not on a rectangle corner floating beside the bumper. **`clearances()` deliberately stays on the OBB**: every demo threshold is calibrated against its readings, and a conservative sensor is the safe side. `tools/hull-check.mjs` guards all of it — it binary-searches the first position the *game itself* calls a touch (through `resolveCollisions` and the `_touch` latch, not through helper functions) over 23 headings against a wall and a parked car, fails above 1 cm, and also drives into a wall at three speeds to prove the smaller shape did not open a way through. A hit is counted on the *start* of contact, latched per obstacle in `o._touch` (reset in `loadLevel`/`restart`) — counting while overlapping gave a stationary car on a kerb 18 hits in 10 s. Soft-obstacle drag is `exp(-2.5*dt)`, not a per-substep constant. HUD clearances (front/rear/left/right, blind zones) come from ray casts `rayOBB`/`castMin` in `clearances()`, capped at `SENS_MAX` (3 m): five rays per side **including the body corners**, plus one diagonal per corner folded into both adjacent readings. Perpendicular-only rays were blind to obstacles sitting diagonally off a corner and overstated the gap by up to ~0.9 m; the remainder (~0.23 m worst case) is the honest blind spot a real parking sensor has. **Demo segment thresholds (`frontLt`/`rearLt`/`leftLt`/`rightLt`) are calibrated against these readings** — changing the sensor model silently breaks every demo that uses them, since a condition already true at segment entry ends that segment instantly and the whole chain cascades. `clearances()` also returns `corner` — the single body corner with the least room (three rays each, below `CORNER_WARN` = 1.2 m), computed here rather than at draw time because `emitCornerPosts` also runs in the mirror passes. It carries `CORNER_KEEP` hysteresis (0.08 m): alongside a wall every corner on that flank reads the same gap, and without a bias toward the already-lit corner the highlight flips between them every frame.

**Showing where the car ends** (the player's own complaint: "не видно габаритов"). `opt.refs` is a three-position level, not a toggle — key `B` cycles it, `REFS_NAMES` names it, `trainer_refs` persists it: `0` as in real life, `1` the ground frame plus corner posts, `2` adds the distance marks and blind-zone lines (`#blindCell` follows level 2). Mobile defaults to 1. **`emitCornerPosts()` must not be gated on `camMode`** — it was cockpit-only, which is exactly why the chase camera the player actually uses showed no extremities at all. The ground frame in `drawRefs()` is drawn on the same footprint the collision uses (`CAR_HULL`, see **Collision & clearances**), so it cannot drift from what a hit is measured against; front edge yellow, rear orange, and the nearest corner turns red with a distance pill from `drawMarkLabels` (which claims its `labelBoxes` slot first, ahead of the phase markers). The hood-corner whiskers in `emitCarMesh` brighten only for the player's car — `lights` is what separates it from the parked ones.

**Levels**: `LEVELS` array, 32 built-in entries plus the player's own pads appended at load (1–13 манёвры двора, 14–19 дриллы габаритов, 20–22 экзаменационные упражнения: эстакада/задний ход/остановка у тротуара, 23–26 город на собственной геометрии: повороты/разворот/пешеходный/уступи, 27–31 город на общей карте: светофор/перестроение/разворот с трамвайных путей/кольцо/косой перекрёсток, 32 экзамен-маршрут). Each level is `{ name, task, tip, steps[], refs, hacks[], transfer, build(), marks()?, phases[]?, coach(state)?, examRoute()? }`:
- `build()` returns `{ obs, dec, start, goal, ramps?, city? }` — obstacles from factories `wall/kerb/hedge/pcar/cone/post/sign/actorCar`, road decals from `stripe/circleDec/roadDec/crossDec/zebraDec/stoplineDec`, plus `start` pose and a `goal` rectangle with angle tolerance (`goalPoseOk` + stopped = `goalReached`, held ~0.55 s). **`goalPoseOk` requires ALL FOUR body corners inside the rect** — city-level goal zones are w=3.0/l=5.6 because demo «морковки»-погоня leaves 0.2–0.4 м of lateral offset; a 2.6-wide zone silently never triggers.
- `phases[]` (replaces `coach` on converted levels) — stateless if-chain: `phaseTick(dt)` each frame picks the first entry whose `when(s)` is true; a phase carries `hint` (full-sentence fallback text), `marks` (names resolved from `marks()` into `p._marks` at `loadLevel`), and optional `mirror` ('left'/'right'/'center' — glows in cockpit view). `s` is `curS`: pose, `gear`, `vel`, `steer` plus the four live clearances (`front/rear/left/right`). `coach(s)` remains the fallback for levels without phases (level 9 only).
  - **Structured card fields** (all 12 phase levels carry them): `icon` (direction glyph ⬆/⬇/⏹/🅿), `act` (short action ≤ ~45 chars — what the card shows), `goal` (`{metric:'ang'|'front'|'rear'|'left'|'right', target, dir:'up'|'down'}` for the live progress chip, or `{text}` for landmark goals), `wheel` (`lockL/lockR/left/right/straight` → mini steering pictogram), `move` (`fwd/rev/stop` → target-gear badge), `why` (long explanation, rendered by the disclosure block). `hint` stays as the fallback: the card renders it when `act` is absent, custom pads and «Полигон» work unchanged. `phaseTick` passes the whole object through, so extra fields are safe. **Chip targets come from the demo segment that leg mirrors** (`dist/frontLt/rearLt/…`), never from a marker of a different leg — L1's «до жёлтой линии» text contradicted the demo's `dist:0.5` and drove the tail into the kerb; the number lives in the chip, not in the prose.
  - A phase switch needs the candidate to hold for `PHASE_HOLD` (0.22 s). Without it the hint flickers, because stepping the selector P→D passes through R and `car.gear` goes negative for a frame.
  - **Chains must cover every reachable state and stay monotone along the maneuver.** Traps hit in practice: a coverage gap makes a later unrelated phase win (guard forward phases with `gear>=0`, end with an unconditional entry); keying purely on heading makes the chain run *backwards* at the end of the maneuver, when the angle sweeps back through the same band; and a reverse phase with no positional bound fires when the player backs up far from the pocket. Discriminate the legs by a value that only moves forward — lateral position `u` in the parallel levels — bound them to the stretch where the maneuver happens, and normalize to 0…360 (`(deg(angNorm(th))+360)%360`) wherever the heading crosses ±180°, as in levels 2 and 3.
  - **Phase thresholds must equal the drawn marker positions.** A hint that says "stop here" 0.6–1 m before the line the player can see teaches the wrong reference point; when a phase mirrors a demo step, take its boundary from that segment's target (level 3's приёмы switch at 148°/212°/258° because the demo drives to 145°/205°/262°).
  - The `P`/`N` selector notices never replace the phase hint — they prefix it ("⏸ P · … Дальше: …"). Without that the very first instruction of a level was a lecture on the gear selector and nothing about what to do, so a new player just sat still.
  - **Every hint is a pair: action + the sign it is finished** ("веди, пока «угол к цели» не дойдёт до 45°", "назад до 0,3 м по «зазоры сзади»", "до голубой стоп-линии"). A hint that only names the action leaves the player overshooting the point they were aiming for. The termination sign must reference something visible on screen — that is why the HUD carries an **«угол к цели»** readout (heading minus goal axis, coloured by the level's tolerance): hints referenced a 45° angle that the panel never showed.
  - Three teaching layers sit *above* the phase hint in `updateHUD`'s priority chain, because each answers a question the phase hint can't: `selWarn` (why the shift was refused), `game.hitMsg` from `hitReason()` (which corner hit what; the geometry lesson now lives in `game.hitWhy` and renders as the card's disclosure block), and `goalMiss()` (why standing in the zone isn't a pass). Two guards keep that layer honest: `goalMiss` is only *shown* when the car is nearly stopped or the pose is already correct — on the move mid-maneuver «стоишь под 50°, доверни» fired exactly when 45° was the goal; and its advice must name the movement direction and the protruding side («сзади лишь 0,1 м — подай вперёд, забирая левее» — computed from body corners in the goal frame), because an addressless «вкатись глубже» drove a literal-minded learner straight into the neighbour. The angle case gets the phase's `act` appended as «Дальше: …»; the self-contained cases must not — the phase next to them may say the opposite («остановись») and the pair reads as a contradiction.
  - **The coach card** (`coachCard(kind, icon, main, goal, ph)` in the HUD section): every branch of the priority chain maps to a kind — `k-stop` (refused shift, red), `k-hit` (collision, orange), `k-info` (toast), `k-almost` (goalMiss, yellow), `k-gear` (P/N, blue), `k-act` (phase), `k-ok`, `k-demo` — coloured left edge + icon, so message types are tellable apart at a glance. The card's `innerHTML` is rebuilt only when a composite key changes (`coachKey`); dynamic strings go through `textContent` (pad names are user input). **Text contract: the card's `textContent` must read as a plain sentence** (spaces between blocks) — the scripted learner and screen readers consume it. The goal chip shows «→ цель X · сейчас Y» + a progress bar from the value at phase entry (`coachGoalTick`, per-frame writes only on change); tapping the chip pulses the corresponding `#bar` cell (`cellPulse`) — this is what ties the hint's words to the HUD numbers. `goalMiss()` is called once per frame and cached — it used to be evaluated twice in the old ternary chain.
  - **Progressive disclosure**: a phase's `why` renders as a collapsible block. Auto-expanded for the first 3 launches (`trainer_runs`, incremented once per session in `doAct('start')`), then behind the «?» button / `Slash`. The toggle is an XOR against the auto-default — a plain OR made the block impossible to collapse for a novice. On `compact` the «?» is hidden; tapping the card toggles it with a 4 s auto-collapse (`#coach` gets `pointer-events:auto` only via `.haswhy` there). Key names in hints say `Enter`, never «.» — on ЙЦУКЕН the physical Period key types «ю» and the literal advice didn't work; `Slash` was free for disclosure for the same reason.

Demo geometry is exact: stop points and arc angles are derived from full-lock rear-axle radius R≈3.84 м (lateral shift of a two-arc parallel park = 2R(1−cosφ)); don't tune them by eye, recompute. Three calibration lessons paid for in this file: погоня-`aim` at a far point NEVER converges laterally — use a chain of near «морковок»; a left U-turn's heading goes NEGATIVE (target `th:rad(-176)`, not +178 — `angNorm` puts the miss at 356°); a sensor-based end condition true at segment ENTRY (e.g. `rightLt` while passing a parked row) ends the segment instantly and каскадом рушит цепочку — prefer geometric ends (`uGte/vGte/th`).

**Keyboard vs. text input.** The `keydown` listener is global, so `typingInField(e)` must skip it whenever focus is in an input — key codes are physical, so a Cyrillic "к" arrives as `KeyR` and used to restart the level while the player was typing a pad name. Modal states filter commands too: `pressKey` passes only `K`/`Delete`/`Backspace` while the editor is open (a digit key used to swap the level out from under it), and `doAct('resume')` re-shows the win screen instead of closing it, because `game.done` freezes physics and a closed overlay left no way out.

**A demo must not touch anything, and its carrots decide whether it does.** `demoAim` is pure pursuit, so the car always cuts inside the line joining the carrots — the roundabout chain handed a segment over 45–70° of arc before the next carrot and the path fell to radius 8.07 against island kerbs whose edge is at 8.25, riding them for half the run. Carrots on one radius every 30°, each segment ending ~3 m short of its own carrot on whichever coordinate is monotone there (u along the bottom of the ring, v up the right side), hold the path at 9.74. Two more rules paid for on the same demo: a carrot placed exactly on the target lane centre is not enough on the exit — the car arrives at it still angled and lays its outer flank on the kerb, so the first exit carrot goes a metre PAST the lane centre; and a turn signal set more than ~60° of heading before the maneuver self-cancels on the way (`stepCar` watches heading, not the wheel), so on a ring the blinker segment must be the one right before the exit. The parallel park had the same class of defect one metre wide: stopping 20 cm too far forward before the maneuver put the nose into the neighbour's rear corner during the second arc. `tools/level-audit.mjs` fails the run on any contact — it latches `o._touch`, because `satMTV` after the frame sees nothing (a solid obstacle is already pushed out).

**A demo must survive its own level's detectors.** The headless regression only proves the maneuver is drivable — it never runs `violationsTick`, so a показ can commit the very violation the level exists to teach and every player following the green ideal line collects the same penalty. `tools/demo-vio.mjs <индексы>` replays each demo in real time with the detectors dosed in parallel (a clean level prints an empty code list) and `tools/yield-sweep.mjs <индекс>` walks the level's own ideal path against the actor with a growing start delay, printing the minimum separation and the codes for each — that sweep is how `YIELD_TCA/GAP/LAT` were set. Replaying each city demo that way found four: level 23's demo drove the whole middle street on the ONCOMING half (`aim` at v=+1.65 instead of −1.65, a hard strike on an образцовый проезд), level 25 paused for narration with its tail on the zebra, level 27 ran a red, level 31 was fined by the yield detector for a legal pass. Re-run that probe after touching a city demo, a detector zone or a detector threshold.

**Testing the teaching, not just the code.** The demo regression proves the maneuvers are physically solvable, but a demo replays hardcoded segments and never reads a word of the UI — it passed every level while a real player would have sat still in `P`. The check that finds those defects is a scripted "learner": an agent that reads `#coach` each frame and does only what the text says. Run it after changing hints. `tools/level-audit.mjs` covers the same ground without the learner's own driving errors: it replays every level's demo headlessly and calls `phaseTick` per frame, producing the exact tape of «which card was up, for how long, and how far its chip's bar got». That is what found a level with no live hint at all (the six габарит-drills showed the `genericCoach` fallback «Свободная площадка» while driving), a phase chain that plays backwards after the maneuver is over (level 29 told the player to signal and start the U-turn again while parked in the goal zone), a chip whose bar never moves, and `updateHUD` throwing every frame. Its chip criterion is the **player-visible** one — `pct >= 100` exactly as `coachGoalTick` computes it — because a `target:0` angle chip never satisfies a strict `v <= 0` and a stricter tool cries wolf on every phase. Exit 1 on: a page exception, a level whose card is empty, a level with neither `phases` nor `coach`.

**Feel-the-extremities drills** (`precision` on a level, `drill:true` for the level-picker section): the goal is a *gap*, not a rectangle. `goalReached` branches on `precDef()`; `precGap()` reads the trained side's clearance, or the level's own `measure()` when the target is something the sensors cannot see (a painted line is not an obstacle). **The trained side's HUD number is replaced by `?` until the car stops** — this is the mechanism, not decoration: with the real number on screen the player aims at a digit instead of learning the distance. Stopping *is* the answer (`precTick`, 0.6 s hold, gated on `game.moved` so the initial standstill in `P` doesn't count), and the attempt reveals once — `precShown` stays set until `restart()`, otherwise creeping forward a centimetre at a time buys unlimited free feedback. `precision.th`/`thTol` guard the sides, where a car parked askew shows the same 20 cm on its nearest corner without actually being parallel. **`side` is optional and every consumer must treat it so**: the level whose target is a painted line has only `measure()`, and the `?`-masking line in `updateHUD` read `$({front:'cf',…}[p.side])` unguarded — `$(undefined)` is null, and level 18 threw a TypeError 120 times a second. Everything below that line in `updateHUD` (the coach card, «угол к цели», blind zones, the gear and blinker badges, `frameCost`/`perfTick`/`qTick`) simply stopped running on that level, and the card kept the previous level's text; `render` sits above it, so the world still drew and the level looked alive. Each drill also carries a one-entry `phases` chain: the reveal itself is already handled by `goalMiss()` → `precReport()`, which outranks the phase hint, so one unconditional card carrying the instruction is the whole chain. Tuning check that matters: a scripted run of random stopping positions passes ~1 % of the time — a drill that random stops can pass teaches nothing.

**Progress** (`progAdd`/`progOf`/`progLine`, keyed by level name): runs, clean runs and best time per level, written in `win()`. Drills instead record `bestErr` in centimetres plus `bestBlind` — the same error achieved with `opt.refs===0` — because on a precision drill time is the wrong measure and rewarding it pushes the player to hurry and miss. Shown on the level-select cards and the win screen. It exists because a trainer needs a repetition loop — without a visible record there is no reason to drive the same level twice, and repetition is where the skill actually forms.

**HUD layout**: `#coach` lives in the band between `#topleft` and `#topright` (fixed `left`/`right` + `margin:0 auto`) so it can't cover them. The in-cabin mirror is drawn on the canvas, so `render` publishes its bottom edge as the `--mirbot` CSS variable and compact-mode `#bar` positions itself below it. The `.sidebtn` buttons stay in the 102–167 px band, above the side mirrors (whose top is `clamp(H*0.40, …)` — they never start higher while the buttons are visible), or an HTML button silently swallows taps aimed at a canvas mirror. `updateHUD` runs every frame, so all its DOM writes go through `setText`/a cached `car.sel` — writing `innerHTML` or `textContent` unconditionally costs a style recalc per frame. While an overlay is open, arrow keys and Space are left to the browser so a long task list can be scrolled; `Escape`/`H` close the task and start overlays but deliberately not the win screen, which is the only way out of `game.done`. The touch tutorial (`#thelp`) pauses the game while it is up, so the level timer doesn't run while it is being read. **The touch bottom band is width-budgeted, and the budget must be recomputed whenever anything in it changes.** It holds 14 targets across the screen: steering (3), `#tmenubtn`, `#tgear` (4 gears + 2 turn-signal arrows), `#trestart`, drive (3). `#tmenubtn`/`#trestart` are offset from the centre by **half of the whole `#tgear` row**, not half of the gear letters — the old ±101 was computed for four 44 px letters while the row has six tiles (6·44 + 5·5 = 289, half 144.5), so ≡ and ⟲ lay exactly on the arrows and the turn signals could not be pressed at all on a phone, silently failing the blinker ritual that the city/exam detectors score. At 667 px (SE landscape) the band does not fit at stock sizes, so a **width** media query (`max-width:760px`) shrinks every element — the compact block above it keys on `max-height:420px` and never catches that screen. `mt-check` asserts zero overlap over 40 px² and no target under 36 px on four phone sizes in both gearboxes. Glyphs: the arrows are `◀ ▶` (U+25C0/25B6), never `⯇ ⯈` (U+2BC7/2BC8) — the system font stack has no coverage for the latter and draws them as a stack of bars; nobody noticed for months because the buttons were covered.

**Cameras**: chase / top-down / cockpit (`opt.camMode`, cycled with C, V jumps to cockpit). Mirrors render in every mode (`opt.mirrors`, key Z); in cockpit the camera sits at the driver's eye and `camInsideCabin()` adds the modelled interior. Mouse/pointer drag orbits, wheel zooms.

**Shadows**: every obstacle casts one, not just cars. The offset comes from `LIGHT` itself — `SHADOW_K = LIGHT.x/LIGHT.y` (0.419 per metre of height), applied as `(du, dv) = (+K·h, −K·h)`; the polygon is the convex hull (`hull2`) of the footprint and its offset copy. Obstacles never move, so `shadowPoly` runs once per obstacle in `loadLevel`/`edRebuild` into `o._shadow` and the frame only fills it; the player's `carShadow` is the one computed live. Before this the car's shadow was a hand-tuned 0.34 m offset tied to neither the body height nor the light direction — it fell where nothing was lit from.

**Sightline rule**: everything in the cabin that is not a window must stay **below the hood line** as seen from `EYE` — the hood edge (`HOOD_Z/HOOD_Y`) is at −8.9°, so the wheel rim top (centre 0.90, rim top 1.085 → −12.7°) and the binnacle hood (1.06 → −12°) sit under it, and the roof header (1.39, bottom 1.36 → +17.5°) above the windshield. With the wheel centred at 1.01 the rim covered the road 7–30 m ahead and the owner «did not see where to drive». Check with a cockpit screenshot after moving anything on the dash.

**Driver's eye**: `EYE = {lat:-0.36, y:1.22, z:-0.20}` (body frame) feeds both `fpEye` and `blindZone`, so the cockpit camera and the «не видно перед / за» numbers can never disagree; with the eye at `z=0, y=1.27` the roof header was 0.26 m ahead and the A-pillar 0.39 m away and both rendered as slabs across the view. The level tips quote the blind distance (5,4 м) — recompute them if `EYE`, `HOOD_Z/Y` or the rear sill change. Default cockpit FOV is 62° vertical (`opt.fpFov`). The windshield gets a faint tint quad plus a dark sun strip (alpha colours: a 4th component in `col` makes `shadeCol` emit `rgba`), sorted with a negative `bias` so the wheel and dash draw over it.

**Car body**: `CAR_ST` is a 13-station loft with a 12-point section (`stationPts`: sill chamfer, lower flank, shoulder `ys` = widest point, belt `be`, tumblehome to `wg`, crowned roof/hood to `wr`/`yt`); edge sets `EDGE_SILL/EDGE_SIDEGLASS/EDGE_TOPGLASS` decide colour per station kind (`bump/body/glass/cabin/pillar`). The windshield runs z 0.30→0.85, so the dash pad, A-pillars, door cards and the floor extend to z 0.84–0.86 and the wipers sit on the cowl at z 0.96. Wheel arches are dark half-discs on the flank with `bias` 0.3, like the seams and handles — the flank itself stays solid. Collision/HUD geometry comes from `CAR`, not from the loft, so reshaping the loft never touches physics or demos; only `HOOD_Z/HOOD_Y` (hood edge) feed `blindZone`. Exterior look (2026-09-06): `MAT.paint/glass/chrome/plastic` go through the outdoor branch of `shadeCol` — `refl` mixes in the sky (`SKY_REF`, stronger on up-facing normals), `spec/shin` is a sun highlight from the view vector, `emit` (lamps) skips the lambert term; `sectionNormals` gives every loft section outward vertex normals once, and cars within 16 m get gradient-shaded flank faces (`n1/n2`, side edges only — roof/hood normals lean along z and the section doesn't know it), beyond 40 m no material at all (a `pow` per face showed in JS time). Wheels are 12-segment tyres with a chrome disc, five dark spoke windows and a hub; grille and plates are `img` faces (`carGrilleCanvas`, `plateCanvas`).

**Cabin lighting**: `cabinLit` (set around `emitInterior`, cleared in a `finally` so it can never leak into outdoor faces) switches `shadeCol` to interior lighting — ambient 0.48 (0.38 turned the dash into a black mass), no fog, a height-based occlusion term that darkens toward the floor, and the light comes **from the windshield** (`cabinLight`, set per frame in `emitInterior` from `fwd(car.th)` + up), not from the sun, so the dash and wheel look the same at any heading. On top of that sits the material model: `pushFace(v,n,col,bias,o)` takes `o = {mat, ao, n1, n2, img}`. `MAT` (matte/softtouch/leather/satin/gloss/cloth/rubber) is the *reflectance*, never the colour: a Blinn-Phong highlight from the view vector (`spec/shin`; leather `shin` is 6, not 14 — with six tube facets a narrow highlight falls between the edge normals and never shows), `ao` multiplies the ambient in niches (door pocket, handle cup, rear headliner), `n1/n2` are shading normals at the edges v0–v3 and v1–v2 and turn the face into a `createLinearGradient` — this is how a five-face roll, a six-sided pillar or the 24×6 wheel torus read as smooth; the geometric normal `n` still does the back-face cull, a shading normal there would drop silhouette faces — and `mat.grain` lays a procedural seamless tile (`grainPattern`: leather/cloth/rubber, periodic value-noise) through `ctx.transform` at `GRAIN_PX` 700 px/m. The tile coordinates come from the **body frame**, not from the face's own edges: `grainUV` (called from `pushFace` while `cabinFrame` is set) projects the face's v0/v1/v3 onto one of three body planes chosen by the dominant component of its body-space normal, and `grainFace` solves the affine map from those coordinates to the screen. With per-face U/V every part started the pattern at its own corner under its own angle, and headliner, roof rail and pillar each ran the weave a different way — the trim read as patchwork («текстуры криво друг на друга накладываются»). The plane choice cannot flicker: the normal is constant in the body frame. A degenerate affine (edge-on face) skips the pass. The earlier single screen-space noise pass read as monitor static, not as trim. Both extra passes skip near-clipped faces (their edge points are not the real corners). `MO` holds the common `o` presets (`pillar` = cloth × 6 sides, `round` = matte × 8, `stalk`). Instruments are the counter-exception: the speedometer needle, the wheel marks, the dome lens and the blinker repeaters go through `emitLit()`, which drops the interior mode for their faces, because under the cabin dimming they sank into black — losing the very readouts added to make the steering angle and gear readable. The dial itself is an `img` face (`dialCanvas(sel)`: four cached canvases, current gear letter green, scale 0–60 shared with the needle through `DIAL_A0/DIAL_SPAN/DIAL_MAX`, gear row in the upper half because the hub hides the lower); its backing quad is cabin-shaded `CAB.DARK`, so the image square does not stand out on the binnacle. `tools/cockpit-shots.mjs` (playwright-core from a temp dir, see its header) shoots five head poses plus chase, runs `sortAudit` on 8 yaws, the demo regression, `frameCost` and (with `--sweep`) the head-turn mode-toggle count, and exits 1 on any failure — run it after touching the cabin: desktop ≈3 ms JS, phone landscape ≈3 ms.

**Cabin**: `emitInterior` is a thin caller over `cabinCtx` (shared `P`/`D`/`quad`/`panel`/`strip`/`at`/`box` helpers in body-frame `lat, y, z`, all taking the `o` material object last; `panel(pts,col,out,n,m,o)` subdivides both ways — the floor is a 5×3 grid because a full-width strip's centroid was nearer than the seat cushion standing on it; `strip(pts,col,out,n,normalAt,o)` sweeps one edge with per-edge normals from `normalAt(t)` — `emitDash`'s `sweep` builds the rolled top pad and the cluster hood from a `(y, z, ny, nz)` profile, the armrest is the same profile along z; `DET` is the bias for door-card attachments; `pushBar(P,a,b,r,col,seg,bias,o)` cuts the roof header into 4 pieces so its centroid stays above the mirror hanging under it, and `o.sides` (6/8) makes pillars, header, stalks and column round) plus `emitCabinShell` / `emitCabinRear` / `emitDash` / `emitWheel` — split because looking forward, looking over the shoulder, and glancing at the instruments need different parts and get edited separately. It is drawn only where `camInsideCabin()` is true, which already excludes the side-mirror passes (their cameras sit at `lat ±1.04`, outside the `|lat|<0.95` test) — no extra flag is needed for that, and the whole cockpit frame costs ≈2.8 ms of the 11 ms budget. Things that are easy to get wrong here: the wheel's tilt basis makes `b2` point **down**, so the top of the rim is at angle `−π/2` (the "12 o'clock" mark sits on the tube's driver side at `TOP`, the grips at `TOP±36…76°`, and the three spokes radiate downward, which is what leaves the top open); the instrument cluster has to sit *above* the hub or the wheel hides it; the gear letters live in the dial image because the console lever is below the driver's field of view; and two thin details that nearly touch on screen (glovebox seam vs door seam, vent ring vs its slats) must differ in `bias` in the right order or `sortAudit` flags the pair — bias means «nearer», so a seam lying on a panel gets 0.015, never `DET`.

**Adaptive performance**: the governor watches the **real rAF interval** (`frameGap`, EMA), not the JS time — Canvas rasterization is invisible to JS, and the old `frameCost`-based `dprCap` logic kept Retina resolution on a software-canvas browser running at 13 fps. `QUALITY[0..8]` is the ladder of sacrifices in order of cost/visibility: grain off → DPR 1.5 → gradients off + car materials off (`cars`) → DPR 1.25 → DPR 1.0 + small details off (`detail`: vents, buttons, seams, handles, wheel torus 12×4) + world `maxD` 55 → DPR 0.8 → 0.65 → 0.5 (rendered smaller and stretched by the browser); the `cars/detail/maxD` fields exist because on a phone the frame is JS-bound (3300 faces ≈ 16 ms at CPU×4) and pixels alone don't help — level 5 cuts JS to ≈10 ms; `qTick` is silent for the first 3 s (load-time stutter once dropped the level and the ratchet locked grain off for good), then steps down after 1 s above 19 ms, steps up after 6 s below 17.5 ms with JS < 6 ms, 2 s cooldown between steps, and a ratchet (`qBest`) never returns to a level that proved slow until reload — without it the loop oscillated «good 6 s / bad 1 s». `faceMode` reads the level; `frameCost` still drives the trail budget. Measured on a software Canvas 1440×900@2: q0 14 → q1 25 → q2 40 → q3 48 → q4 63 fps; GPU stays at q0/120 fps. Mirrors render into their own canvases (≤ 1.5 DPR) one per frame round-robin (the dragged one every frame) and are blitted mirrored — three full scene passes per frame cost more than the cockpit itself; `ctx` is a `let` swapped for the mirror's context inside `renderMirrorInto`. `?perf=1` shows fps · p95 · JS ms · DPR · level · faces (DOM write twice a second). `tools/perf-bench.mjs` measures fps/p95/ops per frame in a windowed Chrome, GPU or `--disable-gpu`, with `OLD=<ref>` to compare against a commit, `FORCE_Q`/`SETTLE`/`ONLY` for the ladder — never the owner's own browsers. Keep this in mind before adding per-frame allocations or heavy draws.

**Turn signals**: `car.blink` (`'L'|'R'|null`) is event-driven — `setBlink` fires from `pressKey` (`Q`/`E`; the camera nudge that used to sit there moved to `[`/`]`), never polled from `input`, because `demoStep` clears the whole `input` map every frame. Self-cancel lives in `stepCar` and watches the **heading** (≥60° turned since activation + wheel near zero), not the wheel — «разворот в три приёма» crosses wheel-zero many times and would false-cancel. Phases carry `blinker:'L'|'R'` (card shows a blinking arrow chip), demo segments carry `blink`. Handbrake `KeyJ` (X is taken by «руль в ноль»): holds on any grade; throttling with it engaged strangles accel to a crawl — the mistake is felt, not messaged. HUD badges (`#blinkers`, `#handbrake`) are written only on state change.

**Slope / эстакада**: zones in `level.ramps` (`rampZone`/`deckZone` factories, compiled with local axes + bb). Physics is one gravity term in `stepCar` **before** the mode branches, so brake and P cancel it in the same substep; creep honestly loses only to grades ≥16 % (`creepAccel` 1.5 vs g·sinθ) — the training ramp uses 0.17. `car.roll` is the rollback meter (exam threshold 0.3 м), reset when the car moves with its gear. Rendering: `toCam` warps **every vertex** by `groundH(-x, z)` behind the `RAMP_ON` fast-path — world, shadows, decals and marks climb consistently; the deck is painted by flat decals (`drawRampDecks`) that the warp lays onto the slope. The ramp is a structure on flat ground — `screenToGround` and collisions stay 2D. **The car is the exception: it is a rigid body and must not be warped vertex by vertex.** While the nose is on the 0.17 grade and the tail is still flat, per-vertex `groundH` shears the loft and the cabin diagonally, and from the cockpit the dash — a metre ahead of the eye — rises 0.17 м *above* it and stands across the view (the owner's «машина от первого лица пропадает в текстурах»); at the deck edge `groundH` is discontinuous, which is where the black shards came from. So `carRampSync()` computes one frame per render — deck height under the rear axle plus the pitch from the height difference between the axles — and `carLift(u,v)` raises everything attached to the car by that plane: `toCam` switches to it while `carRampUse` is set (around `emitCarMesh`/`emitInterior`/`emitCornerPosts` in `drawSceneInto`), and the cockpit eye, both side mirrors, the centre mirror and the chase target all read the same function. **`camInsideCabin` measures height from the deck under the camera**, not from world zero — with the raw `cam.pos.y` the 1.56 м ceiling test failed halfway up the ramp, the interior stopped being emitted at all, and the cockpit showed the bare loft from inside.

**City layer**: two generations live side by side. **Per-level geometry** (levels 23–26): factories `roadDec/crossDec/zebraDec/stoplineDec/sign` — roads and markings are decals; знаки are obstacles (kind `'sign'`: post + `pushFace` shield shapes with a light-raised normal — the honest horizontal normal shaded the enamel to 42 % and made signs unreadable). **A sign's `yaw` is the direction its face points (`fuv(yaw)`), so it must be the OPPOSITE of the driver's heading** — levels 23–26 were all placed with the driver's own heading and showed the grey back panel, which is what «почему знак повёрнут не к нам лицом» meant. In the shield's own frame `+lat` runs to the VIEWER'S LEFT (`R = rgt(yaw)`), so any asymmetric pictogram is mirrored unless that is accounted for. Detail smaller than ~0.15 м on a 0.74 м shield is invisible at the distance a sign is read from: «СТОП», the pedestrian and the three ring arrows are drawn once into a canvas (`signPic`) and mapped onto the shield as an `img` face, whose quad must fit entirely inside the shape beneath it (the quad's own fill is opaque). Only the bent «главная дорога» line stays geometry — two convex quads, because one concave polygon collapsed to an invisible sliver. Check with a head-on shot of all seven shields, never by reading the code. **The unified city** (levels 27–31 and the exam): `cityWorld(CITY_SPEC)` compiles a declaration of nodes and roads into `{obs, dec, city, nodes}` — 8 nodes and 16 streets over ~200×175 m. A node's asphalt patch is the convex hull (`nodeDec` + `hull2`) of its arms' mouths, so a plain cross, a T, a skew Y (60°/120°), an offset pair and a roundabout need no separate factories, only different coordinates; the square `crossDec` is kept only for 23–26. `roadDec2(dec,u,v,yaw,len,r)` draws a street of `r.lanes` per direction with an optional tram bed and returns lane meta; `roadEdges` gives the lane boundaries in the road's local axis (`laneSolid` decides which are solid — `solid:'center'` only the centre line, `solid:'all'` every inner boundary, used on the approach to a junction where changing lanes is already forbidden). Asphalt is cut into 42 m chunks overlapping by 6 cm — butt-joined chunks left a light seam along the street, and one 200 m rectangle was filled whole every frame. `tramDec` lays two tracks (gauge 1.524) down the middle and returns meta into `city.trams`; `roundDec` paints the ring, and `cityWorld` puts an **octagon of kerbs** on the island — OBB collision cannot express a circle, and without a barrier both the demo and the player drove straight across it. `buildRenderList` passes `sign`, `light` and `guide` through **unsplit** — segmentation copies only base fields and drops `pic`/`group`/`rows`. **`cityWorld` also publishes the declaration as a graph** — `city.graph = {V, adj, E}`, named nodes plus free street ends, edges carrying `name`/`yaw`/`len`/`hw`/`lanes`/`tram` — because `build()` returns only `{obs,dec,city,…}` and dropped `nodes`/`radius` on the floor. On it sit `citySnap` (nearest street, or the ring node itself when the point is inside a roundabout — otherwise a car on the ring snapped to some arm and the route line jumped behind it through the island) and `cityRoute(from, to, {side})` → `{pts, len, legs}`, Dijkstra over 8 nodes and 17 edges (≈0,2 мс, safe to call several times a second). The path is laid **in the travel lane**, not on the centre line: rightmost as in life, leftmost before a left turn, the tram bed before a U-turn, with the lane change spread over the last `LANE_SHIFT` = 26 м so the line also says *when* to move over. The lane formula is confirmed by three authored numbers it reproduces exactly — the exam start pose u=8.15 on Ленина, the Садовая pocket at v=−68.35, and the Заводская lane meta. Roundabouts get a real arc (`ringArc`, radius `round−3.5`) **counter-clockwise in (u east, v north)** — right-hand traffic keeps the island on the left; checked against the routes themselves (enter from the south, «второй съезд» exits west; enter from the west, «первый съезд» exits south). **Street-name signs** (`guideSign`/`emitGuide`/`guidePic`, kind `'guide'`): the names had been sitting in `CITY_SPEC` unused since the city was written, so «направо на Садовую» named a street nothing in the world identified. One 6.10.1 plate per junction arm, on the driver's right, facing them (`yaw` = the arm's outward heading), rows «arrow + street» sorted by relative bearing; content derived from the graph, so T, skew Y and the offset pair need no special cases. Two rules paid for: the plate stands **beyond the kerb** (`hw + 1,2 м`) because the demos of levels 27–31 drive this same city and `level-audit` fails on any contact, and **roundabout nodes get no plate** — left/straight/right arrows lie there, exits are counted around the ring. `streetName(r)` trims the author's suffix after « · » (`'Заводская · подъезд к кольцу'`) — the driver needs the street. **`drawDecals` culls by distance** from the camera (`camGroundUV`) against the same `maxD` as obstacles; `decBounds` computes each decal's bounding circle once in `loadLevel`/`edRebuild`. Without it the whole city was filled every frame: at the city edge culling keeps 47 decals of 210, and on a throttled phone JS drops 5.01 → 4.74 ms. **Traffic lights**: `trafficLight(u,v,yaw,group,offset)` + `lightPhase` — a 24 s cycle (green 10, yellow 2, red 12) computed **from `game.t`**, so demos and `computeIdealPath` replay frame for frame; `'NS'` and `'EW'` are half a cycle apart. `lightStops` treats yellow as prohibiting. `emitTrafficLight` draws the active lens with `MO.emit` (no lambert) — a lens turned away from the sun went black exactly where the signal decides whether to stop. Meta objects are collected into `build().city{stoplines,zebras,oncoming,turnZones,yieldZones,lanes,trams,rounds,lights}`; the violation detectors (`violationsTick`) run off that geometry, are silenced during demos (a demo would fine its own показ) and never run headless (frame isn't called there). Detector rules paid for: a stop line needs a `_seen` latch (the car must have been *behind* it) — `s>0.3` is true on the first frame for any line already passed, and starting a level beyond one fined you instantly; a stop line that carries a `light` is skipped by the plain detector, because on green stopping is wrong; the lane detector is silent inside a `uturn` zone, since a 180° arc crosses every lane and turned each legal U-turn into «перестроение без поворотника»; `tram-turn` samples «on the tracks?» at the moment the **arc starts** (heading 15° off the entry course), not continuously — a U-turn from the right lane crosses the tracks anyway; and a `yieldZone` only fires while the car is **moving**, or a player who honestly stopped at the line still got a непропуск. **A yield is judged by the moment of closest approach, never by plain distance**: the detector projects both cars forward at their current velocities (`YIELD_TCA` 3.5 s) and fines only when at that moment the player sits in front of the other car's nose (`−0.5…YIELD_GAP` 4 м) and inside its lane (`|lat| ≤ YIELD_LAT` 2.4 м). A «closer than N metres» test fined the player for the very car they had honestly let past — on the roundabout it stays within 10 м for seconds after crossing — and a bare closing-distance test fined the оblique side-by-side pass on level 31. Calibrated by sweeping the level's own ideal path against the actor with a growing start delay: penalty below ~3.8 м of separation, silence above. `oncoming` rects are direction-aware (the same half of the road is legal after a U-turn) and must not cover a U-turn zone — mid-arc the heading is still within the 0.4 dot-product gate; two `turnZones` on one crossing need `dir` (entry heading). Statists: `actorCar(u,v,yawDeg,col,wps,speed,trig)` — waypoint-follow in `actorsTick` (called in `frame` before the physics substeps, speeds ≤2 м/с), **live shadow** through the `carShadow` path (the per-level `_shadow` cache would leave the shadow at the spawn pose), `_hitByPlayer` latch feeds `collision-actor`. **An actor must never be parked on a demo's path**: `computeIdealPath` replays the demo headlessly and `actorsTick` is not called there, so the car becomes a stationary obstacle — level 30's roundabout demo stalled for 28 s against it. Phases that wait for a statist gate on `actorNear(u,v,d)`, otherwise the card holds «стой» forever.

**Провал попытки** (`strict:true` on levels 23–31; the exam scores points instead): a gross violation cancels the whole run, the way it does in life. `PENALTIES` carries two flags — `fatal` (collision with a car/wall/sign/actor, непропуск, red light) ends the attempt immediately, `hard` (встречка, стоп-линия, сплошная) is a strike: the first shows a red `k-stop` card naming the rule, the second fails. `attemptVio`/`failAttempt` live next to `examPenalty` but never both fire — `vio()` routes to the exam first. `hitPenalty` is the single entry for collisions and is **muted during demos** (the показ would burn the player's attempt). **A kerb touch is counted but is not a strike**: a city street is 6.6 m wide with a metre of clearance each side, and a novice will scrape one — cancelling the run for that drives away exactly the player the trainer is for. `levelFailHTML` shows cause + the rule + the log + an «отработать приём» button from `EXAM_TRAIN`; `doAct('resume')` re-shows it, because `game.done` freezes physics.

**Exam** (level 32, marked by `def.examRoute()`, built on the unified city): `PENALTIES` per the 2026 reglament — 5/3/1 баллов, fail at `EXAM_FAIL_SUM`=7, аварийные (`collision*`, `yield`, `redlight`) are `fatal` immediately. `examPenalty` is fed by `vio()`, fresh collisions in `resolveCollisions` (actors excluded — the detector covers them) and `examTick` (rollback >0.3 м, driving with the handbrake). **Two modes** (`opt.examMode`, `trainer_exammode`, ≡ menu and the briefing): «тренировочный» keeps the full HUD, clearances, parking beep and the corner route map, announces the command ahead of time and *debriefs* a violation on the card instead of ending the run; «настоящий» is the silent inspector — `examInit` disables marks/guides/trails **in memory** (never through `pressKey` — it persists to localStorage), clearances render `'—'`, `parkBeep` is silent, and a gross error fails. `examTeardown` at the top of `loadLevel` restores. **Navigation**: a stage is `{cmd, short, at, turn, done(curS)}`; `examNav` picks the wording by **distance along the route** (`exam.leg.dist`, `Math.hypot` lied on every bend and that number is what the player uses to decide when to move over) — «Через 60 м — направо» → «направо» → the full command — and the card shows the direction glyph. Without it the player learned about a maneuver after passing it, which is what «в экзаменационном ничего не понятно» meant. `examBriefHTML` shows the route map and the command list **before** the start (`examRouteSvg` draws the asphalt decals plus the stage points); `drawRouteMap` repeats it in the corner in training mode — `drawMinimap` cannot be reused, it is centred on the car at a fixed 4.7 px/m. **Three routes** of 9–10 stages (≈365–515 m), each with two internal randomisations, covering both regulated crossings, the tram U-turn, the lane change before the solid line, the roundabout, the skew node and the ramp; stop-stages are stateful closures counting standstill frames. The pocket is squeezed from the other side too — **it must not land inside the next maneuver's zone**: the southern pocket's second draw sat at v=−38, inside the U-turn zone (0,−35 · l 18 → v ∈ [−44,−26]) and 3,2 м short of the U-turn point, so the player stopped at the kerb already in the zone and got «развернись» with no room to reach the tram tracks. Two more things the U-turn needed, and the reason the owner said «не понимаю, когда там разворачиваться»: the `short` form must carry the part that says *where* («разворот с трамвайных путей», not «разворот») — the full command is visible only beyond 75 м and again inside 10 м, and the lane change happens in the gap between; and `examMarks()` puts an `mpoint` beacon on the current stage's `at`, cached by stage number because `drawSceneInto` calls it per frame per mirror pass. Before that split a pass in «тренировочный», with markers, clearances and an on-the-spot debrief, went into the same counter as a real one, so «сдал экзамен» meant nothing; **«готов» now requires a pass in the real mode on each of the three routes** (`examRuns()`), because three passes of one memorised route prove nothing — `exam.routeId` comes from `examRoute()` tagging the array it returns. `examNextHTML` says on the win screen what the run was worth and what is left. because that store is keyed by **level name** and the name carries the number, `PROG_RENAME` migrates the exam's old key once — inserting levels 27–31 would otherwise have wiped every player's exam record. `examReadiness()` = % of clean runs over `EXAM_SET` (17 levels), shown on the start screen and level picker with `weak` card highlighting, with the real-mode route count on a second line. The **corner route map and the briefing draw the same routed polyline** as the asphalt line (`examMapPath`, cached per run) with street labels, the car as a heading triangle and the stage's turn glyph — the old map joined the command points with straight dashes across the blocks and answered nothing. It earns its keep — it is what caught the two level defects this layer was built on: the Заводская `oncoming` rect west of Ленина marked the **southern** half as oncoming for eastbound traffic, i.e. the player's own lane (the very lane the «перестройся вправо» stage requires with `s.v<-3.3`), costing 5 баллов and a red card on two routes of three — the exam has no demo, so `demo-vio.mjs` had never driven that zone.

**learner.js** (repo root, not deployed): the scripted «ученик» — reads the coach card every 100 ms **structurally** (`.cm` action text, `.cw` wheel pictogram, `.cgear` target gear, `.cg` goal chip with its `done` class and «цель X° · сейчас Y°» numbers) plus the HUD «угол к цели» (`#angVal`) and clearances, and does only what they say. Run in the page console: `const stop = learnerStart()`; `window.learnerLast` shows the branch it took on the last tick. It tests the **teaching texts**, not the physics. Stop-words are searched only in the action text — the chip «стоп у голубой линии» is a landmark, and reading it as a command parked the learner at the start line forever. Composite cards («Ты в зоне, но … Дальше: …», the P-note) are followed by their «Дальше:» part; «доверни/подровняй» comes from the prefix. Novice rules the text never states but every maneuver needs: reverse at a crawl, turn the wheel to lock at a standstill (the demo brakes while it turns too — winding from lock while rolling adds ~20° of arc), stop-and-shuffle in a tight pocket, back off after a touch, straighten under a toast. Traps paid for: JS `\b` is ASCII-only — `/зон[а-я]*\b/` never matches after Cyrillic; the `ang` chip is |heading − goal axis|, so a turn's goal is `target:0, dir:'down'`, not «90 up»; a left U-turn's heading goes 0 → −180 and wraps to +177 — window it from both sides. Hint-chain defects the learner exposed and that are now fixed live in git history (7319f5a, 31cb3c6). Known variance: in the 2.3 m pocket it occasionally times out shuffling the last 5–8°, and on the city levels 28–31 it wanders inside a 6.6 m street — its steering is bang-bang with no lane-keeping, so on a road with a metre of clearance each side it scrapes kerbs and arrives at the goal zone crooked. Treat a city timeout as a signal to read the card chain, not as proof the level is unsolvable: the demo, `detectorCheck` and `routeCheck` cover solvability. It still earns its keep there — it found that «встать в правую полосу» as words steers a novice into the kerb (a goal chip needs a *number*), that a U-turn phase chain without a positional bound loops through the whole circle, that a stopped car was being fined for непропуск, and that a phase chain must cover «проехал зону» or the card tells you to stop where there is no zone. When testing a fresh `index.html` in the browser, load it with a cache-buster (`?nocache=<ts>`) and check `fn.toString()` for the edit's marker — the static server's heuristic caching once had a whole test pass confirm stale code.

**Лендинг: пять вопросов посетителя.** Страница обязана отвечать на пять вопросов по порядку
их чтения: что это, кому и в каких ситуациях, какой результат, почему стоит смотреть дальше,
что сделать сейчас. Аудит 17.09.2026 нашёл, что из пяти закрыты были два — и оба провала были
структурными, а не текстовыми.

**Прикреплённый герой не имеет права молчать.** `.hero` — это «сколько прокрутки уходит на
показ»: `park.js` считает её как `hero.offsetHeight − stage.offsetHeight`, а копия (H1, лид,
кнопка) уходит уже на 10 % этой прокрутки. При 240vh после первого экрана шло 1260 px, то есть
1,4 экрана без единой строки текста, заголовка и цифры — кто не понял с первого кадра, уходил
здесь. Высота и длина ленты реплик — **связанная пара**, и считается она в пикселях прокрутки
на реплику: порог читаемости 110 px на десктопе и 90 px на телефоне, примерно один щелчок
колеса. Второй, более злой дефект того же места: `paint` раскладывал прогресс линейно по точкам
пути, поэтому доля прокрутки у реплики равнялась **длине её участка траектории**. Замер показал
«Точку 2» (доворот на 45°, главный ориентир всей методики) с 30 px из 675, а «Готово» не
показывалось вовсе — прокрутка кончалась раньше. Лечится не высотой, а равными окнами на реплику
(`indexAt`/`STEP_SPAN`). Гоняй `tools/hero-tape.mjs` после правки высоты `.hero`, списка `STEPS`
или разбивки `PATH` по фазам: он печатает px на каждую реплику и молчащую прокрутку и падает на
пробитом пороге. Глазами это не проверяется — сам знаешь, что написано в карточке, и успеваешь
прочитать то, чего новичок не увидит. Точность замера стоила двух правок самого инструмента:
границы реплик уточняются делением до 1 px, и каждая позиция прокрутки ждёт **двух кадров
rAF**, а не таймера — `park.js` перерисовывает сцену внутри `requestAnimationFrame`, и с
фиксированными 10 мс одна и та же сборка давала минимум от 101 до 110 px при пороге 110.
Флаки-гард хуже отсутствующего: он учит не доверять проверке.

**И у героя есть второй режим — `prefers-reduced-motion`.** Там `paint` ставит `open = 1`
сразу, а `.hero.open` гасит копию: H1, лид и кнопка «Начать тренировку» были невидимы, на
первом экране оставались стоящая машина и карточка «Готово», единственным входом — «Играть» в
шапке. Блок `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` обязан стоять **последним в `<style>`**:
правила для телефона (`max-width:899px`) задают `.hero-coach { top }` ниже по каскаду и при
равной специфичности победило бы то, что написано позже. Возвращать нужно и копию, **и
затемнение** (`.hero-scrim`) — без него текст ложится на машины и на линию траектории, причём
проверка перекрытий по геометрии остаётся зелёной, а читать нельзя. Правку героя смотри в
обоих режимах и на кадре, а не только в цифрах.

**Раздел лендинга без входящих ссылок не существует.** `/avtoshkolam/` — страница второго
читателя из voice-карточки и вход в B2B-майлстоун — провисела сиротой с самого M6: ни шапка, ни
подвал, ни один текст на неё не ссылались, попасть можно было только по прямому адресу. Проверять

**Ссылка внутри текста подчёркивается всегда** (`article a,.card a,.cta-note a,.final-alt a`):
одним цветом её не отличить тем, кто различает цвет хуже, и Lighthouse считает это ошибкой
(`link-in-text-block`, a11y 96 → 100). Кнопки и навигация подчёркивания не требуют.

**Результат называется величиной, которую продукт правда считает** — готовность к экзамену
(`examReadiness`: доля чистых заездов по 17 уровням `EXAM_SET`) и лучшая точность дриллов в
сантиметрах (`bestErr`). Обещать сдачу нельзя: этого продукт не контролирует. Строка доверия на
странице — личная история автора, а не звание: «искал такое приложение и не нашёл, поэтому
написал сам» проверяемо, «аналогов нет» читателю проверить нечем и voice-карточка запрещает
сравнение через отрицание.

**Landing clips**: `tools/landing-clips.mjs` records a level's демонстрация in a headless browser and encodes `landing/public/clips/<name>.{mp4,webp}` (h264, no audio, ~100 КБ each, poster via `cwebp` — homebrew's ffmpeg has no webp encoder). Recording happens at 1280×800 and is scaled to 960×600: at 960 the game's HUD falls into its narrow layout and the panels overlap. Service chrome is hidden with an injected stylesheet, never `remove()` — `updateHUD` writes into those nodes every frame — and `coachCard` is patched so the card shows the segment's instructor line instead of «Демо 3/7 · любая кнопка прерывает». A VP9 track is not built: on this material webm comes out twice the size of h264. The page loads them with `preload="none"` and starts playback from an IntersectionObserver, skipping it entirely under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

Грабли, каждая уже оплаченная: **токена бота в `index.html` нет и быть не может** — страница отдаётся всем, да и `connect-src 'self'` в CSP не пустит браузер на `api.telegram.org`; **с РФ-хостинга api.telegram.org недоступен вообще** (замер spark: таймаут 15 с), весь исходящий трафик идёт через `HTTPS_PROXY` на tinyproxy бокса 194, и `NO_PROXY=api.telegram.org` уводит канал в офлайн; новый `location` в nginx **обязан** содержать `include snippets/pozerkalam-headers.conf`, иначе его `add_header` отключает серверные заголовки безопасности; смоук деплоя шлёт `{"dry":true}`, иначе каждый деплой присылал бы владельцу мусорный отчёт; снимок экрана снимается **до `showOv`** — оверлей ставит `paused=true` и кадр перестаёт перерисовываться; сервис отвечает браузеру сразу, а шлёт фоном, и недоставленный отчёт остаётся в journal целиком. `window.BUILD` вписывают оба билд-скрипта — без версии сборки «у меня баг» неотличимо от «у меня старый кэш SW».
