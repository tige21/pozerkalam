# CLAUDE.md

This file provides guidance to Claude Code (claude.ai/code) when working with code in this repository.

## What this is

A browser car-maneuvering trainer (parking simulator) — one self-contained file, `index.html` (~5700 lines): CSS, HTML UI, and a single `<script>` with zero dependencies, no build step, no package.json; git-репозиторий на `main` без feature-веток (плюс генерат `codegraph-src/index.js` для индекса — см. Code Search). All UI text and code comments are in Russian — keep new text and comments in Russian. Testing harness: the headless demo regression (`computeIdealPath` runs on every `loadLevel` — intercept `console.warn` over all `DEMOS` keys, zero timeouts = green) plus `learner.js` (см. ниже) for the teaching texts.

## Architecture of index.html

Layout: CSS first (~190 lines), then HTML panels/overlays/touch controls, then one `"use strict"` script to the end. The script is organized in commented sections (`/* ---------- name ---------- */`) in dependency order; new code goes into the matching section.

**Coordinate system** (comment at ~line 264): all physics runs in the 2D ground plane `(u, v)`; the 3D world maps as `x = -u`, `z = v`, `y = up`. `yaw 0` points along `+v` ("north"), yaw grows turning right. Converters: `fuv`/`ruv` (2D orts) and `fwd`/`rgt` (3D).

**Software 3D renderer on Canvas 2D**: `setCam`/`toCam`/`toScreen` + near-plane clipping (`clipNear`, `NEAR` = 0.08 m — the headliner is 0.16 m above the eye, and the old 0.22 m plane cut it away when looking up, showing sky through the roof; a camera must never sit within 0.08 m in front of geometry it should not see, which is why the side-mirror cameras sit *inside* their housings at the rear face), `screenToGround` for the inverse (it reads the `viewCam` snapshot taken in `render`, because by the time a pointer event fires `cam` holds the last mirror's parameters), and a painter's-algorithm face list — geometry is emitted via `pushFace`/`pushBox`/`pushQuad`/`pushPoly` with lambert shading (`shadeCol`), then depth-sorted and drawn in `flushFaces`. The whole scene renders through `drawSceneInto`, which is reused for the main view and for mirrors (`renderMirror`) in cockpit mode. Painter's-sort rules paid for: (1) the sort key is the **distance from the camera to the centroid of the visible, near-clipped polygon**, not the depth along the view axis — depth changes with every degree of head rotation, so from the cockpit the panel order re-shuffled while looking around and the cabin flickered and «lost» panels; distance is rotation-invariant, and a face crossing the near plane is measured by its clipped part (the plain vertex mean of a wall beside the car went to ~0 and the wall was painted over the cabin); (2) inside the cabin the scene is two passes — world, flush, then own body + interior — because nothing outside can be nearer than the interior trim; mirrors and outside views stay single-pass so the own body sorts against neighbours; (3) a small detail sitting on a big face (exterior door handles and seams, door-card trim) carries a `bias` in metres that sorts it nearer — the host face's centroid is often nearer than the detail — and `pushFace/pushQuad/pushPoly/pushBox/pushBar` all take it; (4) interpenetrating boxes cannot be sorted at all (the seat back drew through the cushion, the visor through the roof header) — cabin parts sit on each other, never inside; (5) long world boxes are split into 3.5 m segments in `buildRenderList` (a 7 m kerb had its centroid nearer than the neighbour's wheel in front of it). `tools/sort-audit.js` → `sortAudit({yaws:[0,-90,135]})` in the page console counts wrong-order overlapping face pairs per pass (`[FIX:sort]`); it found all of the above — run it after touching geometry, the target is 0 in the interior pass. Three more per-frame rules in `flushFaces`/`pathCam`: **faces are not stroked** — `pathCam` pushes every screen vertex outward along the bisector of its edge normals by 0.9 **device** px (`EXPAND_DEV/pxScale` — 0.5 left a light seam on GPU Canvas at DPR 2, two half-covered edges; 0.9 is what the old 0.9 px stroke gave; `pxScale` is the DPR of whichever canvas is current, swapped for mirrors), damped for faces thinner than 1.5 px so edge-on loft slivers don't fatten into lines, orientation from the signed screen area — in screen coordinates (y down) a positive shoelace area is clockwise and `(dy, −dx)` points outward; with the sign inverted every face shrank and the world turned into a wireframe of gaps («всё в линиях»). This closes the anti-aliasing hairlines between neighbours (sky showed through them and shimmered) at zero extra raster passes — the old fill+stroke per face was half the frame time on a software Canvas; faces under 1 px² are skipped (far cars had hundreds). `cockpit-shots` guards it with `skyGap` (sky-coloured pixels in the dash band of the forward frame, live `getImageData`, limit 60; stroke gave 10, expansion 12 headless / 19 GPU, the inverted sign 36); and a cabin face may carry a second `fill()` of the same path after the colour fill — `grainFace` (material grain: a semi-transparent noise tile mapped through `ctx.transform` in the face's own metres→pixels frame, no `clip`, no `overlay` — both were dearer than the fill itself) and `imgFace` (a pre-rendered canvas as a `no-repeat` pattern mapped by three corners: speedometer, console screen, window switches, speaker grille), see **Cabin lighting**. What a face gets is decided by the pure `faceMode(f, s0..s3)`: mirrors are always flat; a gradient needs its two endpoints ≥ 1.5 px apart (a **degenerate `createLinearGradient` paints nothing** — edge-on faces vanished for a frame, «текстуры моргают») and a face ≥ `GRAD_MIN_PX` = 40 px, otherwise the mid colour `colMid`; grain fades continuously with screen area and tile minification (`f.gk`, thresholds would flicker); endpoints come from `toScreenClamped` (depth clamped to `NEAR`) so a face crossing the near plane never jumps between modes. `tools/cockpit-shots.mjs --sweep` turns the head −60…+60° by 1° on three pitches and counts mode toggles and degenerate gradients — the target is 0/0. `pushBar` cuts long bars into `seg` pieces with end caps only at the real ends — a cap on an inner joint showed through the neighbouring piece as a grey post.

**Car physics**: `CAR` const holds all vehicle dimensions/limits (kinematic bicycle model, Ackermann steering — `ackermann`, `sweep`). `stepCar` integrates at a fixed 1/120 s substep inside `frame`. Steering rate is speed-dependent (`steerRateNow`). The canonical pose is the rear axle (`car.ru/rv/th`); body center comes from `bodyPos()`/`setBody()` via `C2R`. Gear selector `P/R/N/D` lives in `car.sel`, drive direction in `car.gear`. `shiftSel` enforces a real automatic's rules: brake-shift interlock (leaving `P` requires `input.back`, i.e. S or Space held) and full stop before P/R/D — both refuse with a HUD warning (`selWarn`, `selBlockT`; touch also highlights the brake button via `body.needbrake`). Space is a second brake key, not a handbrake. `selectGear(target)` walks the lever to a position and reverts to the starting one if it can't get there, so a single command never strands the car in `N`. Four ways to shift, all through those same rules: `Enter` (D ⇄ R — the constant move while parking), `P`, `,`/`.` one position at a time, and clicking the P R N D letters in the HUD (`#gearVal span[data-gear]`) or the touch `#tgear` buttons.

**Self-centering** (the wheel returning on its own) uses the real law — self-aligning torque grows with the *square* of speed: `back = min(casterMax, casterGain*(v² − casterV²))`, gain `rad(2.2)` per (m/s)², cap `rad(50)`. Keeping the `casterV` threshold at 2.2 m/s is what makes the feature safe: below ~8 km/h there is no return at all, so parking maneuvers, the level hints that say "hold full lock", and the calibration of all 11 demos are untouched, while at 15–19 km/h the wheel unwinds in 0.7 s. Measured: 9 km/h → 11 s to unwind, 11 km/h → 3.7 s, 14 km/h → 1.4 s, 19 km/h → 0.7 s. **A linear-in-speed law cannot be used** — it already unwinds the wheel at parking pace and breaks parallel parking. `demoStep` holds the wheel only while `|err| > 1.5°` and *brakes in that same condition*, so a caster that drifts the wheel inside the dead band would make demos stutter between "correcting" and "driving": the narrow 0.35° trim band exists for that, and it is gated on `|vel| > casterV` — widening it below the threshold changes the arc radius and rips up the segment calibration (it cost L11 a failed run before being gated).

Full lock is 34.5° at the wheels = **1.49 turns** of the steering wheel (`steerRatio` 15.5); `atLock()` marks the stop, which colours the rim mark red and clicks `tone()` **on the edge** (`lockWas`), never per frame.

**Collision & clearances**: OBB SAT with minimum translation vector (`satMTV`, `resolveCollisions`). A hit is counted on the *start* of contact, latched per obstacle in `o._touch` (reset in `loadLevel`/`restart`) — counting while overlapping gave a stationary car on a kerb 18 hits in 10 s. Soft-obstacle drag is `exp(-2.5*dt)`, not a per-substep constant. HUD clearances (front/rear/left/right, blind zones) come from ray casts `rayOBB`/`castMin` in `clearances()`, capped at `SENS_MAX` (3 m): five rays per side **including the body corners**, plus one diagonal per corner folded into both adjacent readings. Perpendicular-only rays were blind to obstacles sitting diagonally off a corner and overstated the gap by up to ~0.9 m; the remainder (~0.23 m worst case) is the honest blind spot a real parking sensor has. **Demo segment thresholds (`frontLt`/`rearLt`/`leftLt`/`rightLt`) are calibrated against these readings** — changing the sensor model silently breaks every demo that uses them, since a condition already true at segment entry ends that segment instantly and the whole chain cascades. `clearances()` also returns `corner` — the single body corner with the least room (three rays each, below `CORNER_WARN` = 1.2 m), computed here rather than at draw time because `emitCornerPosts` also runs in the mirror passes. It carries `CORNER_KEEP` hysteresis (0.08 m): alongside a wall every corner on that flank reads the same gap, and without a bias toward the already-lit corner the highlight flips between them every frame.

**Showing where the car ends** (the player's own complaint: "не видно габаритов"). `opt.refs` is a three-position level, not a toggle — key `B` cycles it, `REFS_NAMES` names it, `trainer_refs` persists it: `0` as in real life, `1` the ground frame plus corner posts, `2` adds the distance marks and blind-zone lines (`#blindCell` follows level 2). Mobile defaults to 1. **`emitCornerPosts()` must not be gated on `camMode`** — it was cockpit-only, which is exactly why the chase camera the player actually uses showed no extremities at all. The ground frame in `drawRefs()` is drawn on the same OBB the collision uses, so it cannot drift from what a hit is measured against; front edge yellow, rear orange, and the nearest corner turns red with a distance pill from `drawMarkLabels` (which claims its `labelBoxes` slot first, ahead of the phase markers). The hood-corner whiskers in `emitCarMesh` brighten only for the player's car — `lights` is what separates it from the parked ones.

**Levels**: `LEVELS` array, 27 built-in entries plus the player's own pads appended at load (1–13 манёвры двора, 14–19 дриллы габаритов, 20–22 экзаменационные упражнения: эстакада/задний ход/остановка у тротуара, 23–26 город: повороты/разворот/пешеходный/уступи, 27 экзамен-маршрут). Each level is `{ name, task, tip, steps[], refs, hacks[], transfer, build(), marks()?, phases[]?, coach(state)?, examRoute()? }`:
- `build()` returns `{ obs, dec, start, goal, ramps?, city? }` — obstacles from factories `wall/kerb/hedge/pcar/cone/post/sign/actorCar`, road decals from `stripe/circleDec/roadDec/crossDec/zebraDec/stoplineDec`, plus `start` pose and a `goal` rectangle with angle tolerance (`goalPoseOk` + stopped = `goalReached`, held ~0.55 s). **`goalPoseOk` requires ALL FOUR body corners inside the rect** — city-level goal zones are w=3.0/l=5.6 because demo «морковки»-погоня leaves 0.2–0.4 м of lateral offset; a 2.6-wide zone silently never triggers.
- `phases[]` (replaces `coach` on converted levels) — stateless if-chain: `phaseTick(dt)` each frame picks the first entry whose `when(s)` is true; a phase carries `hint` (full-sentence fallback text), `marks` (names resolved from `marks()` into `p._marks` at `loadLevel`), and optional `mirror` ('left'/'right'/'center' — glows in cockpit view). `s` is `curS`: pose, `gear`, `vel`, `steer` plus the four live clearances (`front/rear/left/right`). `coach(s)` remains the fallback for levels without phases (level 9 only).
  - **Structured card fields** (all 12 phase levels carry them): `icon` (direction glyph ⬆/⬇/⏹/🅿), `act` (short action ≤ ~45 chars — what the card shows), `goal` (`{metric:'ang'|'front'|'rear'|'left'|'right', target, dir:'up'|'down'}` for the live progress chip, or `{text}` for landmark goals), `wheel` (`lockL/lockR/left/right/straight` → mini steering pictogram), `move` (`fwd/rev/stop` → target-gear badge), `why` (long explanation, rendered by the disclosure block). `hint` stays as the fallback: the card renders it when `act` is absent, custom pads and «Полигон» work unchanged. `phaseTick` passes the whole object through, so extra fields are safe. **Chip targets come from the demo segment that leg mirrors** (`dist/frontLt/rearLt/…`), never from a marker of a different leg — L1's «до жёлтой линии» text contradicted the demo's `dist:0.5` and drove the tail into the kerb; the number lives in the chip, not in the prose.
  - A phase switch needs the candidate to hold for `PHASE_HOLD` (0.22 s). Without it the hint flickers, because stepping the selector P→D passes through R and `car.gear` goes negative for a frame.
  - **Chains must cover every reachable state and stay monotone along the maneuver.** Traps hit in practice: a coverage gap makes a later unrelated phase win (guard forward phases with `gear>=0`, end with an unconditional entry); keying purely on heading makes the chain run *backwards* at the end of the maneuver, when the angle sweeps back through the same band; and a reverse phase with no positional bound fires when the player backs up far from the pocket. Discriminate the legs by a value that only moves forward — lateral position `u` in the parallel levels — bound them to the stretch where the maneuver happens, and normalize to 0…360 (`(deg(angNorm(th))+360)%360`) wherever the heading crosses ±180°, as in levels 2 and 3.
  - **Phase thresholds must equal the drawn marker positions.** A hint that says "stop here" 0.6–1 m before the line the player can see teaches the wrong reference point; when a phase mirrors a demo step, take its boundary from that segment's target (level 3's приёмы switch at 148°/212°/258° because the demo drives to 145°/205°/262°).
  - The `P`/`N` selector notices never replace the phase hint — they prefix it ("⏸ P · … Дальше: …"). Without that the very first instruction of a level was a lecture on the gear selector and nothing about what to do, so a new player just sat still.
  - **Every hint is a pair: action + the sign it is finished** ("веди, пока «угол к цели» не дойдёт до 45°", "назад до 0,3 м по «зазоры сзади»", "до голубой стоп-линии"). A hint that only names the action leaves the player overshooting the point they were aiming for. The termination sign must reference something visible on screen — that is why the HUD carries an **«угол к цели»** readout (heading minus goal axis, coloured by the level's tolerance): hints referenced a 45° angle that the panel never showed.
  - Three teaching layers sit *above* the phase hint in `updateHUD`'s priority chain, because each answers a question the phase hint can't: `selWarn` (why the shift was refused), `game.hitMsg` from `hitReason()` (which corner hit what; the geometry lesson now lives in `game.hitWhy` and renders as the card's disclosure block), and `goalMiss()` (why standing in the zone isn't a pass). Two guards keep that layer honest: `goalMiss` is only *shown* when the car is nearly stopped or the pose is already correct — on the move mid-maneuver «стоишь под 50°, доверни» fired exactly when 45° was the goal; and its advice must name the movement direction and the protruding side («сзади лишь 0,1 м — подай вперёд, забирая левее» — computed from body corners in the goal frame), because an addressless «вкатись глубже» drove a literal-minded learner straight into the neighbour. The angle case gets the phase's `act` appended as «Дальше: …»; the self-contained cases must not — the phase next to them may say the opposite («остановись») and the pair reads as a contradiction.
  - **The coach card** (`coachCard(kind, icon, main, goal, ph)` in the HUD section): every branch of the priority chain maps to a kind — `k-stop` (refused shift, red), `k-hit` (collision, orange), `k-info` (toast), `k-almost` (goalMiss, yellow), `k-gear` (P/N, blue), `k-act` (phase), `k-ok`, `k-demo` — coloured left edge + icon, so message types are tellable apart at a glance. The card's `innerHTML` is rebuilt only when a composite key changes (`coachKey`); dynamic strings go through `textContent` (pad names are user input). **Text contract: the card's `textContent` must read as a plain sentence** (spaces between blocks) — the scripted learner and screen readers consume it. The goal chip shows «→ цель X · сейчас Y» + a progress bar from the value at phase entry (`coachGoalTick`, per-frame writes only on change); tapping the chip pulses the corresponding `#bar` cell (`cellPulse`) — this is what ties the hint's words to the HUD numbers. `goalMiss()` is called once per frame and cached — it used to be evaluated twice in the old ternary chain.
  - **Progressive disclosure**: a phase's `why` renders as a collapsible block. Auto-expanded for the first 3 launches (`trainer_runs`, incremented once per session in `doAct('start')`), then behind the «?» button / `Slash`. The toggle is an XOR against the auto-default — a plain OR made the block impossible to collapse for a novice. On `compact` the «?» is hidden; tapping the card toggles it with a 4 s auto-collapse (`#coach` gets `pointer-events:auto` only via `.haswhy` there). Key names in hints say `Enter`, never «.» — on ЙЦУКЕН the physical Period key types «ю» and the literal advice didn't work; `Slash` was free for disclosure for the same reason.
  - **Onboarding tour** (`showOnboard`, gated by `trainer_seen`): 5 sequential callouts over the `#thelp` layer (card → «угол к цели» → зазоры → селектор → демо/⟲), pulsing ring on the live element rect, game paused. Desktop runs it from `doAct('start')`; mobile chains it after the touch-buttons tutorial in `closeTouchHelp`. The backdrop handler routes by `onbActive` — closing the tour must set `trainer_seen`, not `trainer_hint`.
  - **First-drive guide** (`tut`/`TUT`/`tutTick`, gated by `trainer_drive`): four LIVE steps on the coach card — hold brake → Enter (D) → release, feel the creep → throttle to 5 km/h — each `done()` watches real input/car state, advancing only on the actual action; a static «how the automatic works» paragraph demonstrably didn't get novices moving. Runs after the tour (`closeOnboard` → `maybeStartTut`), owns the card slot between `hitMsg` and `goalMiss` (selWarn still outranks it — a refused shift IS the lesson), starts only from an untouched `P` (`!game.moved`), and `restart()` resets `tut.i` because the car returns to `P`.
- `marks()` returns named world-space teaching markers built once per load: `mline` (ground line + label), `mpoint` (pillar beacon), `mspot` (ground circle), `marrow`, `mghost` (ideal-pose car outline, turns green via `ghostOk`), `mcarpin` (pin on own car corner). Rendered by `drawMarks` (geometry before `flushFaces`) + `drawMarkLabels` (screen-space pills, main view only, ≤3 labels, ≤2 on MOB; overlapping pills are pushed upward via `labelBoxes`). Toggle `opt.marks` (key `O`, localStorage `trainer_marks`, default on incl. mobile).
- `hacks[]` render in the task overlay («Лайфхаки»), `transfer` on the win screen («Запомни для реальной дороги»).
- `parallelStreet(gap, side)` builds the shared parallel-parking street (side=-1 mirrors it); `parallelMarks(S)` builds its markers.
- `DEMOS` holds scripted per-level demonstrations (18 of the 27; slalom and the exam route have none — the exam's входящие манёвры carry their own): segments executed by `demoStep`, which synthesizes `input` presses. Segment fields: `g` gear, `s` steer (-1..1), `aim` point steering (`demoAim`, works in reverse), `blink:'L'|'R'|null` (applied at segment entry — city demos must themselves show the ритуал or the detectors would fine them), end conditions (`th/dist/uGte/uLte/vGte/vLte/frontLt/rearLt/leftLt/rightLt/goal/time`), speed caps `slow` (~5 km/h) / `vmax`, and `say` — narration text shown in `#coach`; before a `say` segment the car brakes for `sayT` (~1.9 s) so it can be read, and active marks pulse. A `goal:true` segment brakes as soon as `goalPoseOk()` — otherwise the demo overshoots the zone. Demo geometry is exact: stop points and arc angles are derived from full-lock rear-axle radius R≈3.84 м (lateral shift of a two-arc parallel park = 2R(1−cosφ)); don't tune them by eye, recompute. Three calibration lessons paid for in this file: погоня-`aim` at a far point NEVER converges laterally — use a chain of near «морковок»; a left U-turn's heading goes NEGATIVE (target `th:rad(-176)`, not +178 — `angNorm` puts the miss at 356°); a sensor-based end condition true at segment ENTRY (e.g. `rightLt` while passing a parked row) ends the segment instantly and каскадом рушит цепочку — prefer geometric ends (`uGte/vGte/th`).
- **Level bounds** come from the start pose and the goal, widened by the obstacles — never from obstacles alone: a pad with no obstacles produced an inverted range and the clamp in `stepCar` threw the car a billion metres away.
- **Custom pads** (`ED_KINDS`, `customToDef`, `loadCustomLevels`): the player's levels are appended to `LEVELS` at startup, so level select, "next level", the minimap and everything else work unchanged. They carry `custom:true` and a `slot` (`-1` for the unsaved "try" draft, which is reused rather than appended again). They have no `phases`, `marks` or `DEMOS` — `phaseTick`, `computeIdealPath` and the demo button already degrade to null, and `genericCoach` supplies the live hint (distance and bearing to the goal, needed heading change, nearest clearance). Stored in `localStorage` under `trainer_levels` as up to 5 slots; `customValid` rejects a malformed slot with a `console.warn` instead of breaking startup.
- **Editor** (`editor` state, `openEditor`/`closeEditor`): a top-down authoring mode with physics paused. `edRebuild()` compiles the item list into a live `level` so the normal renderer draws it; `drawEditor()` adds the grid, the start/goal gizmos and the selection box. Pointer input is routed to `edPointer` before the camera-orbit handlers, and placement uses `screenToGround`. `edValidate()` blocks play/save when the start sits inside an obstacle, the goal is covered, or the goal is too close to the start.
- **Ideal trajectory**: `computeIdealPath()` re-runs the level's demo headlessly at `loadLevel` (state saved/restored, `say`/hits/sound suppressed, ~5 ms) and samples the body path every 0.35 м; `buildIdealDraw` splits it into forward/reverse polylines with direction chevrons (`level.idealDraw`), drawn by `drawIdealPath` under the markers (green = forward, orange = reverse). No per-frame allocation. Gated on `opt.guides`, not `opt.marks` — trajectory lines (prediction arcs, wheel trails, ideal path) all default OFF and toggle via `G`/`T`: on a first launch they buried the teaching markers in clutter; the markers (`opt.marks`) stay on.

**UI**: overlays are HTML strings (`startHTML`, `helpHTML`, `winHTML`, `showTask`) shown via `showOv`/`hideOv`, which also toggle `body.ov` (hides the floating side buttons), with `data-act` buttons dispatched in `doAct`. Keyboard goes through `pressKey(code)` — touch buttons, the menu and the floating buttons reuse it, so new shortcuts added there work everywhere. Floating controls: `#restartBtn` + `#demoBtn` on desktop (`.sidebtn`, hidden in compact/hud-off/overlay) and `#trestart` on touch. With 13+ levels the digit keys no longer cover the list, so `L` opens a card grid (`levelPickHTML`); `K` opens the pad editor, which hides the whole driving HUD via `body.edit`. `restart()` stops a running demo — every path that resets a level (key `R`, the buttons, «Повторить» on the win screen) goes through it, otherwise the demo keeps driving the car while the player thinks they are in control. Touch mode (`MOB`) is auto-detected and persisted; three HUD modes cycle via `cycleHud`. `localStorage` keys: `trainer_hint` (touch tutorial seen), `trainer_seen` (HUD onboarding tour seen), `trainer_drive` (first-drive guide passed), `trainer_runs` (launch counter — auto-expands the card's «why» for the first 3), `trainer_hud`, `trainer_touch`, `trainer_marks`, `trainer_refs`, `trainer_mirrors`, `trainer_levels` (custom pads), `trainer_progress`, `trainer_gearbox` ('AT'/'MT'). `toast(msg, secs)` writes a short settings notice into the coach line (`note`/`noteT`), ranked below `selWarn` so it never buries a refused shift.

**Keyboard vs. text input.** The `keydown` listener is global, so `typingInField(e)` must skip it whenever focus is in an input — key codes are physical, so a Cyrillic "к" arrives as `KeyR` and used to restart the level while the player was typing a pad name. Modal states filter commands too: `pressKey` passes only `K`/`Delete`/`Backspace` while the editor is open (a digit key used to swap the level out from under it), and `doAct('resume')` re-shows the win screen instead of closing it, because `game.done` freezes physics and a closed overlay left no way out.

**Testing the teaching, not just the code.** The demo regression proves the maneuvers are physically solvable, but a demo replays hardcoded segments and never reads a word of the UI — it passed every level while a real player would have sat still in `P`. The check that finds those defects is a scripted "learner": an agent that reads `#coach` each frame and does only what the text says. Run it after changing hints.

**Feel-the-extremities drills** (`precision` on a level, `drill:true` for the level-picker section): the goal is a *gap*, not a rectangle. `goalReached` branches on `precDef()`; `precGap()` reads the trained side's clearance, or the level's own `measure()` when the target is something the sensors cannot see (a painted line is not an obstacle). **The trained side's HUD number is replaced by `?` until the car stops** — this is the mechanism, not decoration: with the real number on screen the player aims at a digit instead of learning the distance. Stopping *is* the answer (`precTick`, 0.6 s hold, gated on `game.moved` so the initial standstill in `P` doesn't count), and the attempt reveals once — `precShown` stays set until `restart()`, otherwise creeping forward a centimetre at a time buys unlimited free feedback. `precision.th`/`thTol` guard the sides, where a car parked askew shows the same 20 cm on its nearest corner without actually being parallel. Tuning check that matters: a scripted run of random stopping positions passes ~1 % of the time — a drill that random stops can pass teaches nothing.

**Progress** (`progAdd`/`progOf`/`progLine`, keyed by level name): runs, clean runs and best time per level, written in `win()`. Drills instead record `bestErr` in centimetres plus `bestBlind` — the same error achieved with `opt.refs===0` — because on a precision drill time is the wrong measure and rewarding it pushes the player to hurry and miss. Shown on the level-select cards and the win screen. It exists because a trainer needs a repetition loop — without a visible record there is no reason to drive the same level twice, and repetition is where the skill actually forms.

**HUD layout**: `#coach` lives in the band between `#topleft` and `#topright` (fixed `left`/`right` + `margin:0 auto`) so it can't cover them. The in-cabin mirror is drawn on the canvas, so `render` publishes its bottom edge as the `--mirbot` CSS variable and compact-mode `#bar` positions itself below it. The `.sidebtn` buttons stay in the 102–167 px band, above the side mirrors (whose top is `clamp(H*0.40, …)` — they never start higher while the buttons are visible), or an HTML button silently swallows taps aimed at a canvas mirror. `updateHUD` runs every frame, so all its DOM writes go through `setText`/a cached `car.sel` — writing `innerHTML` or `textContent` unconditionally costs a style recalc per frame. While an overlay is open, arrow keys and Space are left to the browser so a long task list can be scrolled; `Escape`/`H` close the task and start overlays but deliberately not the win screen, which is the only way out of `game.done`. The touch tutorial (`#thelp`) pauses the game while it is up, so the level timer doesn't run while it is being read.

**Cameras**: chase / top-down / cockpit (`opt.camMode`, cycled with C, V jumps to cockpit). Mirrors render in every mode (`opt.mirrors`, key Z); in cockpit the camera sits at the driver's eye and `camInsideCabin()` adds the modelled interior. Mouse/pointer drag orbits, wheel zooms.

**Shadows**: every obstacle casts one, not just cars. The offset comes from `LIGHT` itself — `SHADOW_K = LIGHT.x/LIGHT.y` (0.419 per metre of height), applied as `(du, dv) = (+K·h, −K·h)`; the polygon is the convex hull (`hull2`) of the footprint and its offset copy. Obstacles never move, so `shadowPoly` runs once per obstacle in `loadLevel`/`edRebuild` into `o._shadow` and the frame only fills it; the player's `carShadow` is the one computed live. Before this the car's shadow was a hand-tuned 0.34 m offset tied to neither the body height nor the light direction — it fell where nothing was lit from.

**Sightline rule**: everything in the cabin that is not a window must stay **below the hood line** as seen from `EYE` — the hood edge (`HOOD_Z/HOOD_Y`) is at −8.9°, so the wheel rim top (centre 0.90, rim top 1.085 → −12.7°) and the binnacle hood (1.06 → −12°) sit under it, and the roof header (1.39, bottom 1.36 → +17.5°) above the windshield. With the wheel centred at 1.01 the rim covered the road 7–30 m ahead and the owner «did not see where to drive». Check with a cockpit screenshot after moving anything on the dash.

**Driver's eye**: `EYE = {lat:-0.36, y:1.22, z:-0.20}` (body frame) feeds both `fpEye` and `blindZone`, so the cockpit camera and the «не видно перед / за» numbers can never disagree; with the eye at `z=0, y=1.27` the roof header was 0.26 m ahead and the A-pillar 0.39 m away and both rendered as slabs across the view. The level tips quote the blind distance (5,4 м) — recompute them if `EYE`, `HOOD_Z/Y` or the rear sill change. Default cockpit FOV is 62° vertical (`opt.fpFov`). The windshield gets a faint tint quad plus a dark sun strip (alpha colours: a 4th component in `col` makes `shadeCol` emit `rgba`), sorted with a negative `bias` so the wheel and dash draw over it.

**Car body**: `CAR_ST` is a 13-station loft with a 12-point section (`stationPts`: sill chamfer, lower flank, shoulder `ys` = widest point, belt `be`, tumblehome to `wg`, crowned roof/hood to `wr`/`yt`); edge sets `EDGE_SILL/EDGE_SIDEGLASS/EDGE_TOPGLASS` decide colour per station kind (`bump/body/glass/cabin/pillar`). The windshield runs z 0.30→0.85, so the dash pad, A-pillars, door cards and the floor extend to z 0.84–0.86 and the wipers sit on the cowl at z 0.96. Wheel arches are dark half-discs on the flank with `bias` 0.3, like the seams and handles — the flank itself stays solid. Collision/HUD geometry comes from `CAR`, not from the loft, so reshaping the loft never touches physics or demos; only `HOOD_Z/HOOD_Y` (hood edge) feed `blindZone`. Exterior look (2026-09-06): `MAT.paint/glass/chrome/plastic` go through the outdoor branch of `shadeCol` — `refl` mixes in the sky (`SKY_REF`, stronger on up-facing normals), `spec/shin` is a sun highlight from the view vector, `emit` (lamps) skips the lambert term; `sectionNormals` gives every loft section outward vertex normals once, and cars within 16 m get gradient-shaded flank faces (`n1/n2`, side edges only — roof/hood normals lean along z and the section doesn't know it), beyond 40 m no material at all (a `pow` per face showed in JS time). Wheels are 12-segment tyres with a chrome disc, five dark spoke windows and a hub; grille and plates are `img` faces (`carGrilleCanvas`, `plateCanvas`).

**Cabin lighting**: `cabinLit` (set around `emitInterior`, cleared in a `finally` so it can never leak into outdoor faces) switches `shadeCol` to interior lighting — ambient 0.48 (0.38 turned the dash into a black mass), no fog, a height-based occlusion term that darkens toward the floor, and the light comes **from the windshield** (`cabinLight`, set per frame in `emitInterior` from `fwd(car.th)` + up), not from the sun, so the dash and wheel look the same at any heading. On top of that sits the material model: `pushFace(v,n,col,bias,o)` takes `o = {mat, ao, n1, n2, img}`. `MAT` (matte/softtouch/leather/satin/gloss/cloth/rubber) is the *reflectance*, never the colour: a Blinn-Phong highlight from the view vector (`spec/shin`; leather `shin` is 6, not 14 — with six tube facets a narrow highlight falls between the edge normals and never shows), `ao` multiplies the ambient in niches (door pocket, handle cup, rear headliner), `n1/n2` are shading normals at the edges v0–v3 and v1–v2 and turn the face into a `createLinearGradient` — this is how a five-face roll, a six-sided pillar or the 24×6 wheel torus read as smooth; the geometric normal `n` still does the back-face cull, a shading normal there would drop silhouette faces — and `mat.grain` lays a procedural seamless tile (`grainPattern`: leather/cloth/rubber, periodic value-noise) through `ctx.transform` from the face's own U/V lengths in metres (`GRAIN_PX` 700 px/m); the earlier single screen-space noise pass read as monitor static, not as trim. Both extra passes skip near-clipped faces (their edge points are not the real corners). `MO` holds the common `o` presets (`pillar` = cloth × 6 sides, `round` = matte × 8, `stalk`). Instruments are the counter-exception: the speedometer needle, the wheel marks, the dome lens and the blinker repeaters go through `emitLit()`, which drops the interior mode for their faces, because under the cabin dimming they sank into black — losing the very readouts added to make the steering angle and gear readable. The dial itself is an `img` face (`dialCanvas(sel)`: four cached canvases, current gear letter green, scale 0–60 shared with the needle through `DIAL_A0/DIAL_SPAN/DIAL_MAX`, gear row in the upper half because the hub hides the lower); its backing quad is cabin-shaded `CAB.DARK`, so the image square does not stand out on the binnacle. `tools/cockpit-shots.mjs` (playwright-core from a temp dir, see its header) shoots five head poses plus chase, runs `sortAudit` on 8 yaws, the demo regression, `frameCost` and (with `--sweep`) the head-turn mode-toggle count, and exits 1 on any failure — run it after touching the cabin: desktop ≈3 ms JS, phone landscape ≈3 ms.

**Cabin**: `emitInterior` is a thin caller over `cabinCtx` (shared `P`/`D`/`quad`/`panel`/`strip`/`at`/`box` helpers in body-frame `lat, y, z`, all taking the `o` material object last; `panel(pts,col,out,n,m,o)` subdivides both ways — the floor is a 5×3 grid because a full-width strip's centroid was nearer than the seat cushion standing on it; `strip(pts,col,out,n,normalAt,o)` sweeps one edge with per-edge normals from `normalAt(t)` — `emitDash`'s `sweep` builds the rolled top pad and the cluster hood from a `(y, z, ny, nz)` profile, the armrest is the same profile along z; `DET` is the bias for door-card attachments; `pushBar(P,a,b,r,col,seg,bias,o)` cuts the roof header into 4 pieces so its centroid stays above the mirror hanging under it, and `o.sides` (6/8) makes pillars, header, stalks and column round) plus `emitCabinShell` / `emitCabinRear` / `emitDash` / `emitWheel` — split because looking forward, looking over the shoulder, and glancing at the instruments need different parts and get edited separately. It is drawn only where `camInsideCabin()` is true, which already excludes the side-mirror passes (their cameras sit at `lat ±1.04`, outside the `|lat|<0.95` test) — no extra flag is needed for that, and the whole cockpit frame costs ≈2.8 ms of the 11 ms budget. The roof rails (`C.RAIL`, headliner edge → door top) exist because without them the sky showed through the gap; A/B pillars are 0.030/0.036 six-sided bars in `PILLAR` with a dark seal bar along the glass — the eye is 0.39 m from the A-pillar and a 0.116 m light bar read as a slab merging with the headliner. The wheel is a torus (24 ring × 6 tube segments, `RT` 0.017, grips `RG` 0.022 set per ring vertex so the step has no gap), three tapered spokes that start 2 mm outside the 12-gon hub and stop 3 mm before the tube, and an airbag pad standing ON the hub with a bias — nothing interpenetrates (painter's rule 4); rim top 1.086 and cluster hood top 1.074 both stay under the −8.9° hood line. Things that are easy to get wrong here: the wheel's tilt basis makes `b2` point **down**, so the top of the rim is at angle `−π/2` (the "12 o'clock" mark sits on the tube's driver side at `TOP`, the grips at `TOP±36…76°`, and the three spokes radiate downward, which is what leaves the top open); the instrument cluster has to sit *above* the hub or the wheel hides it; the gear letters live in the dial image because the console lever is below the driver's field of view; and two thin details that nearly touch on screen (glovebox seam vs door seam, vent ring vs its slats) must differ in `bias` in the right order or `sortAudit` flags the pair — bias means «nearer», so a seam lying on a panel gets 0.015, never `DET`.

**Mirror content**: the centre mirror's camera stands at the interior mirror glass (body z 0.35, y 1.26) looking back with FOV 24° (≈70° horizontal at the 0.30 aspect, a flat mirror) and the cabin IS drawn in it (`camInsideCabin` → two passes): C-pillars, rear header, headrests, rear window — the earlier `noSelf` pass showed only the street and the owner asked why «в зеркале нет салона». Headrest tops stay ≥6 cm below the mirror camera (front 1.195, rear 1.14) or they fill the mirror. The coach card is pinned under the drawn mirror via `--mirbot` (`body.mir #coach`). Side mirrors look 13° outward, 2° down, FOV 46° (was 52°/6°): with that the own flank takes ~1/4 of the width and the **rear door handle sits by the inner edge just below centre** — the reference people set real mirrors by. `emitCarBody` adds door seams and exterior handles (bias 0.3) for cars within 28 m of the camera, never for the car the camera is inside (with the bias the handle top poked through the door card), so neighbours' handles are references too.

**Mirror adjustment**: `opt.mirAdj` holds per-mirror `{yaw, pitch}` **deltas** added to the stock aim in `mirrorCam`, clamped to `MIR_YAW_MAX`/`MIR_PITCH_MAX` (18°/12°) so a mirror can never be lost pointing at the sky; `mirLoad`/`mirWrite` persist them in `trainer_mirrors`, and a malformed value is `console.warn`ed and reset. Dragging on a mirror adjusts it: `mirrorAt()` hit-tests `mirrorRects()` (with a wider pad on `MOB`) in `pointerdown` **before** the orbit branch, otherwise the camera orbit starts in the same frame and drags the view along with it. The horizontal sign is inverted because the mirror image is flipped (`ctx.scale(-1,1)`) — with it, dragging right moves the mirror's view the same way dragging right moves the orbit camera. `MIR_PRESETS` (key `U`, also in the menu) are teaching positions, each carrying the *reason* it is set that way; `out` is applied with opposite signs to the left and right mirrors.

The centre mirror's camera sits *inside* the body, so `drawSceneInto` takes `noSelf` and `renderMirror` passes it for `kind==='center'`: without it the pass draws the car's own shell and interior in front of the lens and the mirror is a black rectangle. The side mirrors keep the car visible — its flank is the parking reference.

**Audio**: WebAudio built from oscillators only (`initAudio`, `engineSound`, `tone`, `thud`, `parkBeep`) — no audio assets. `initAudio` must be called from a user gesture; sound defaults off. `parkBeep` is silent in P/N (`car.gear===0`), like a real car — without that check a car parked next to a wall beeps forever.

**Numbers and drawn references must be derived from `CAR`, not written by hand.** Both kinds of drift have happened: the intro claimed a 2.7 s steering sweep while `lockTime()` showed the real 1.8 s (`maxSteer/steerStill` = 34.5°/19°/s; 0.9 s rolling), and level 9's reference circles were hardcoded at `(-9,0)`, 4.1 m away from where the car's track actually goes — so the level's whole "lay your track onto the circle" mechanic did nothing. That level now computes the turn centre from its own start pose (rear axle + `ruv(th)*sweep(maxSteer).R`), and its demo turns the wheel at a standstill before moving so the arc starts exactly at that centre. Current true values: rear-axle radius 3.84 m (wheel tracks at 3.06 and 4.62), outer body sweep 5.89 m, turning circle 11.8 m. Ghost markers must sit where the demo actually drives — the parallel-park ghost claiming "0.8 m from the row" has to be at `u = 1.1`, not 1.4.

**Adaptive performance**: the governor watches the **real rAF interval** (`frameGap`, EMA), not the JS time — Canvas rasterization is invisible to JS, and the old `frameCost`-based `dprCap` logic kept Retina resolution on a software-canvas browser running at 13 fps. `QUALITY[0..8]` is the ladder of sacrifices in order of cost/visibility: grain off → DPR 1.5 → gradients off + car materials off (`cars`) → DPR 1.25 → DPR 1.0 + small details off (`detail`: vents, buttons, seams, handles, wheel torus 12×4) + world `maxD` 55 → DPR 0.8 → 0.65 → 0.5 (rendered smaller and stretched by the browser); the `cars/detail/maxD` fields exist because on a phone the frame is JS-bound (3300 faces ≈ 16 ms at CPU×4) and pixels alone don't help — level 5 cuts JS to ≈10 ms; `qTick` is silent for the first 3 s (load-time stutter once dropped the level and the ratchet locked grain off for good), then steps down after 1 s above 19 ms, steps up after 6 s below 17.5 ms with JS < 6 ms, 2 s cooldown between steps, and a ratchet (`qBest`) never returns to a level that proved slow until reload — without it the loop oscillated «good 6 s / bad 1 s». `faceMode` reads the level; `frameCost` still drives the trail budget. Measured on a software Canvas 1440×900@2: q0 14 → q1 25 → q2 40 → q3 48 → q4 63 fps; GPU stays at q0/120 fps. Mirrors render into their own canvases (≤ 1.5 DPR) one per frame round-robin (the dragged one every frame) and are blitted mirrored — three full scene passes per frame cost more than the cockpit itself; `ctx` is a `let` swapped for the mirror's context inside `renderMirrorInto`. `?perf=1` shows fps · p95 · JS ms · DPR · level · faces (DOM write twice a second). `tools/perf-bench.mjs` measures fps/p95/ops per frame in a windowed Chrome, GPU or `--disable-gpu`, with `OLD=<ref>` to compare against a commit, `FORCE_Q`/`SETTLE`/`ONLY` for the ladder — never the owner's own browsers. Keep this in mind before adding per-frame allocations or heavy draws.

**Turn signals**: `car.blink` (`'L'|'R'|null`) is event-driven — `setBlink` fires from `pressKey` (`Q`/`E`; the camera nudge that used to sit there moved to `[`/`]`), never polled from `input`, because `demoStep` clears the whole `input` map every frame. Self-cancel lives in `stepCar` and watches the **heading** (≥60° turned since activation + wheel near zero), not the wheel — «разворот в три приёма» crosses wheel-zero many times and would false-cancel. Phases carry `blinker:'L'|'R'` (card shows a blinking arrow chip), demo segments carry `blink`. Handbrake `KeyJ` (X is taken by «руль в ноль»): holds on any grade; throttling with it engaged strangles accel to a crawl — the mistake is felt, not messaged. HUD badges (`#blinkers`, `#handbrake`) are written only on state change.

**Slope / эстакада**: zones in `level.ramps` (`rampZone`/`deckZone` factories, compiled with local axes + bb). Physics is one gravity term in `stepCar` **before** the mode branches, so brake and P cancel it in the same substep; creep honestly loses only to grades ≥16 % (`creepAccel` 1.5 vs g·sinθ) — the training ramp uses 0.17. `car.roll` is the rollback meter (exam threshold 0.3 м), reset when the car moves with its gear. Rendering: `toCam` warps **every vertex** by `groundH(-x, z)` behind the `RAMP_ON` fast-path — car, interior, shadows, decals, marks all climb consistently; car-attached cameras (chase, cockpit, mirrors) add the same lift explicitly, and the deck is painted by flat decals (`drawRampDecks`) that the warp lays onto the slope. The ramp is a structure on flat ground — `screenToGround` and collisions stay 2D.

**City layer**: factories `roadDec/crossDec/zebraDec/stoplineDec/sign` — roads and markings are decals; знаки are obstacles (kind `'sign'`: post + `pushFace` shield shapes with a light-raised normal — the honest horizontal normal shaded the enamel to 42 % and made signs unreadable). `buildRenderList` passes `sign` through **unsplit** — segmentation copies only base fields and drops `pic`. Zebra/stopline factories return meta objects collected into `build().city{stoplines,zebras,oncoming,turnZones,yieldZones}`; the violation detectors (`violationsTick`) run off that geometry, are silenced during demos (a demo would fine its own показ) and never run headless (frame isn't called there). `oncoming` rects are direction-aware (the same half of the road is legal after a U-turn); two `turnZones` on one crossing need `dir` (entry heading). Statists: `actorCar(u,v,yawDeg,col,wps,speed,trig)` — waypoint-follow in `actorsTick` (called in `frame` before the physics substeps, speeds ≤2 м/с), **live shadow** through the `carShadow` path (the per-level `_shadow` cache would leave the shadow at the spawn pose), `_hitByPlayer` latch feeds `collision-actor`.

**Exam** (level 27, marked by `def.examRoute()`): `PENALTIES` per the 2026 reglament — 5/3/1 баллов, fail at `EXAM_FAIL_SUM`=7, аварийные (`collision*`, `yield`) are `fatal` immediately. `examPenalty` is fed by `vio()`, fresh collisions in `resolveCollisions` (actors excluded — the detector covers them) and `examTick` (rollback >0.3 м, driving with the handbrake). The route is an array of `{cmd, done(curS)}` stages with two randomisations (where the «остановка по требованию» happens, which finish pocket); stop-stages are stateful closures counting standstill frames. «Молчаливый инспектор»: the `k-exam` card shows only the current command + score; `examInit` disables marks/guides/trails **in memory** (never through `pressKey` — it persists to localStorage), clearances render `'—'`, `parkBeep` is silent; `examTeardown` at the top of `loadLevel` restores. Violation detectors are checked synthetically: `tools/exam-check.js` → `examCheck()` in the page console drives the car through the exam turn zones with `setBody` + `violationsTick` (right turn / U-turn, with and without a blinker) — the `dir` filter of a turn zone is an *entry* gate only; checking it every frame once dropped every exam penalty for turning without a signal. `examFail` is the game's first losing end: `game.done` + fail screen, the `doAct('resume')` trap re-shows the protocol, Escape during the exam asks «прервать?» instead of help. The protocol embeds an SVG mini-map of `exam.trail` (recorded every 0.35 м in `examTick`) with red dots at penalty positions and «отработать» buttons (`EXAM_TRAIN` maps code→level, `data-act="train:N"`). Progress: `progExam` writes `{n, passed, bestScore}` on top of `trainer_progress` without breaking old records; `examReadiness()` = % of clean runs over `EXAM_SET` (12 levels), shown on the start screen and level picker with `weak` card highlighting.

**МКПП** (`opt.gearbox`, persisted `trainer_gearbox`, toggle in the ≡ menu): `mtDrive` replaces the AT branches of the same longitudinal model. Clutch is **left Shift** (right Shift keeps the look-back); releasing a binary key is made teachable by slowing the release inside the grip zone 0.25–0.75. `car.rpm` mixes free rpm (idle/gas) with wheel-locked rpm by grip; stall at grip>0.45 && rpm<520 → `KeyY` restarts (needs clutch pressed or N). Gears R/N/1/2: `,`/`.` step, Enter toggles 1⇄R, all only with the clutch in; `KeyP` in MT explains «нейтраль + ручник». **Demos stay on АКПП**: `startDemo` and `computeIdealPath` force `'AT'` and restore — every segment is calibrated for creep. Gearbox is switchable from the ≡ menu **and from the start screen** (`gearboxBtnHTML` → `doAct('gearbox')`); both go through `setGearbox`, which saves, restarts the level and toasts the keys, and the start-screen bullet follows the choice. Under the handbrake MT traction is cut to 0.55 — the 1.5–1.6 gear ratios out-pulled the handbrake and the car accelerated to top speed with it on; now it crawls and stalls with the clutch out, like pulling away with the handbrake in real life. `tools/mt-check.mjs` (playwright-core, same setup as `cockpit-shots`) drives the car with **real key events** through 21 rules of the manual box — gear only with the clutch in, stall on a dropped clutch and on stopping in gear, `Y` restart only with clutch or in neutral, pulling away, 1→2, reverse blocked while rolling, handbrake, demos on AT with MT restored, switching back to AT — run it after touching `mtDrive`/`mtShift`/`setGearbox`. It found the handbrake defect; run it against the deployed build with `URL=…` only with the VPN off (the headless browser has no `BindInterface`). Three stalls on the ramp trigger the «попытка сгорела» toast (on the exam each stall is just its 1-балл penalty).

**learner.js** (repo root, not deployed): the scripted «ученик» — reads the coach card every 100 ms **structurally** (`.cm` action text, `.cw` wheel pictogram, `.cgear` target gear, `.cg` goal chip with its `done` class and «цель X° · сейчас Y°» numbers) plus the HUD «угол к цели» (`#angVal`) and clearances, and does only what they say. Run in the page console: `const stop = learnerStart()`; `window.learnerLast` shows the branch it took on the last tick. It tests the **teaching texts**, not the physics. Stop-words are searched only in the action text — the chip «стоп у голубой линии» is a landmark, and reading it as a command parked the learner at the start line forever. Composite cards («Ты в зоне, но … Дальше: …», the P-note) are followed by their «Дальше:» part; «доверни/подровняй» comes from the prefix. Novice rules the text never states but every maneuver needs: reverse at a crawl, turn the wheel to lock at a standstill (the demo brakes while it turns too — winding from lock while rolling adds ~20° of arc), stop-and-shuffle in a tight pocket, back off after a touch, straighten under a toast. Traps paid for: JS `\b` is ASCII-only — `/зон[а-я]*\b/` never matches after Cyrillic; the `ang` chip is |heading − goal axis|, so a turn's goal is `target:0, dir:'down'`, not «90 up»; a left U-turn's heading goes 0 → −180 and wraps to +177 — window it from both sides. Hint-chain defects the learner exposed and that are now fixed live in git history (7319f5a, 31cb3c6). Known variance: in the 2.3 m pocket it occasionally times out shuffling the last 5–8°. When testing a fresh `index.html` in the browser, load it with a cache-buster (`?nocache=<ts>`) and check `fn.toString()` for the edit's marker — the static server's heuristic caching once had a whole test pass confirm stale code.
